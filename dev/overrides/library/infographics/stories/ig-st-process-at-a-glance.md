# Process at a glance

## The process from suppliers to customers
Layout: swimlane sipoc
- Suppliers: Guests, ulta.com and the app
- Inputs: Online orders, store stock
- Process: Route, pick, stage, hand over
- Outputs: Orders ready for pickup
- Customers: Guests who collect in store
> Notes: the example is buy online, pick up in store; replace it with your own process.

## Six steps from order to hand-over
Layout: process chevrons
- Order: placed online or in the app
- Route: to the store with stock
- Pick: items from the shelf
- Stage: bagged at the pickup point
- Notify: the guest gets a ready message
- Hand over: checked and collected

## Who does what at each step
Layout: swimlane handoffs
- Guest: Places the order online
- OMS: Routes orders to a store
- Store associate: All items on the shelf?
- Store associate: If yes, pick and stage the order
- Store associate: If no, offer a substitute
- Guest: Collects the order in store

## The systems behind the process
Layout: hub
- OMS: the order hub
- Store app: picking and staging
- Store stock: what is on the shelf
- Messaging: ready alerts to the guest
- POS: hand-over and returns

## How the process performs
Layout: kpi cards
- Ready time: 2.1 hours (-0.4 h)
- Ready on time: 94% (+3 pts)
- Cancelled orders: 1.6% (-0.2 pts)
> Notes: illustrative figures - replace them with your own.

## Where the process breaks today
| Pain point | What it causes | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Stock wrong on the shelf | Short picks and substitutes | Daily counts of pickup items |
| Picking at busy hours | Orders ready late | A picking slot in every schedule |
| Guest not told | Orders left uncollected | A reminder after [n] hours |
