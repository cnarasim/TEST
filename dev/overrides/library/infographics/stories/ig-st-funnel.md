# Funnel and conversion

## Six in ten bags never become an order
Layout: template funnel
- Visit: guests arrive from search, email and the app
- Bag: product pages, reviews and shade finders fill the bag
- Order: delivery cost and sign-in are where most bags drop
- Loyal: rewards points and refill reminders bring guests back
> Notes: illustrative - each stage's detail says what moves guests on or where they drop.

## One in three buyers buys again
Layout: process pipeline
- Visits: 1.2M (38%)
- Bags: 456K (41%)
- Orders: 187K (34%)
- Repeat buyers: 64K
> Notes: illustrative figures - each stage shows its volume and the share that moves on.
