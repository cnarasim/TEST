# New store opening

## What a new store opening covers
### Purpose
- Open on the planned date, ready to sell
- Give guests a full store on day one
### In scope
- Build-out, fixtures, systems and licenses
- Hiring, training, opening stock and launch
### Outcome
- Opening date met, every shelf full
- A trained team and a working salon on day one

## Many teams, one opening date
Layout: swimlane handoffs
- Store development: Signs the lease
- Store development: Builds out the space
- Merchandising: Sets assortment and opening stock
- Store operations: Hires and trains the team
- Store operations: Ready to open?
- Store operations: If yes, open the doors
- Store development: If no, reset the date

## Systems and data for a store opening
| Step | What the system does | Records it keeps |
|---|---|---|
| Store record | The store is set up with its profile and clusters | Site master, price and tax zone |
| Systems install | POS, handhelds, network and security tested | Device list, test results |
| Opening stock | Assortment and quantities allocated in waves | Allocation, store orders |
| Team | Positions, hires and training tracked | Staffing plan, training records |

## Five phases from lease to grand opening
Layout: process phases
- Build: Permits; Construction; Fixtures (T-26 to T-8 weeks)
- Equip: POS; Network; Security (T-8 to T-4 weeks)
- Staff: Hire; Train; Salon licenses (T-8 to T-1 weeks)
- Stock: Allocation; Deliveries; Set the floor (T-3 to T-1 weeks)
- Open: Soft opening; Grand opening (Week 0)
> Notes: illustrative timing - set the weeks to your own opening calendar.

## Controls and exceptions
| Control | How it works | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| Opening gates | Readiness checked at T-8, T-4 and T-1 weeks | Project lead |
| Systems test | POS, payments and network tested before stock lands | IT store support |
| Licenses | Business and salon licenses posted before opening | Store operations |
| Opening count | Opening stock received and checked before day one | Inventory control |

## KPIs that show it works
| KPI | What it measures | Data source |
|---|---|---|
| Opened on schedule | Stores opened on the planned date | Opening plan |
| Opening cost vs budget | Capital and expense against plan | Project budget |
| Day-one in-stock | Items at presentation on opening day | Store inventory |
| First-year sales | Sales against the store's plan | Sales reporting |
