# Store standards audit

## What store standards audits cover
### Purpose
- Hold every store to one standard
- Fix gaps fast, each with an owner
### In scope
- Floor, back room, safety, salon and cash
- Self-audits, district visits and follow-up
### Outcome
- A comparable score for every store
- Every gap with an owner and a due date

## Audit, act, then check the action
Layout: swimlane handoffs
- Store manager: Completes the weekly self-audit
- District manager: Visits with the standard checklist
- District manager: Scores and photographs gaps
- District manager: Critical gap found?
- Store manager: If yes, fix it the same day
- Store manager: If no, add it to the plan
- Store operations: Tracks actions to closure

## Systems and data for store audits
| Step | What the system does | Records it keeps |
|---|---|---|
| Checklist | One standard checklist on a tablet or phone | Audit template, version |
| Audit | Answers, photos and scores saved by question | Audit result, photos |
| Actions | Each gap becomes a task with an owner and a date | Action item, due date |
| Reporting | Scores by store, district and question | Audit scores, trend |

## Standards improve in a steady loop
Layout: process cycle
- Self-audit: the store manager, weekly
- Visit: the district manager, on a set schedule
- Score: by section, with photos
- Act: owners and due dates
- Verify: follow-up on open actions

## Scores show where to act first
Layout: bullet
- Sales floor: 92 of 100
- Back room: 78 of 100
- Salon: 88 of 100
- Safety: 95 of 100

> Notes: illustrative scores - one district's average by section; replace with your own audit results.

## Controls and exceptions
| Control | How it works | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| Standard checklist | One version for all stores, changed centrally | Store operations |
| Photo evidence | Every failed item needs a photo | District manager |
| Critical items | Safety and cash failures fixed the same day | Store manager |
| Calibration | Auditors score sample stores together each quarter | Regional director |

## KPIs that show it works
| KPI | What it measures | Data source |
|---|---|---|
| Audit score | Average score by store and section | Audit results |
| Audits on schedule | Planned visits and self-audits done | Audit calendar |
| Action closure | Actions closed by the due date | Action items |
| Repeat findings | Gaps found again at the next audit | Audit history |

## Today and target
### Today
- Paper checklists, scores kept in a spreadsheet
- The same findings, visit after visit
### Target
- Mobile audits with photos and automatic scores
- Actions tracked to closure, repeats flagged
