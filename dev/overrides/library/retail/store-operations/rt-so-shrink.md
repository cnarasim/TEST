# Shrink and loss prevention

## What loss prevention covers
### Purpose
- Keep product and cash from walking out
- Act on causes, not only on the shrink number
### In scope
- High-risk items, tags, cases and cameras
- Exception reports, investigations, ORC
### Outcome
- Lower shrink in the highest-risk stores
- Cases closed with evidence and recovery

## Data flags it, investigators prove it
Layout: swimlane handoffs
- Exception reporting: Flags POS and stock exceptions
- Asset protection: Reviews video and records
- Asset protection: Loss confirmed?
- Asset protection: If yes, open a case
- Asset protection: If no, close and tune the rule
- Store manager: Puts the fix in place
- District manager: Reviews shrink and actions

## Systems and data for loss prevention
| Step | What the system does | Records it keeps |
|---|---|---|
| Exception reports | Refunds, voids and discounts scored for risk | Exception alerts by associate |
| High-risk items | Items flagged for tags, cases or counts | High-risk item list |
| Investigation | Video, receipts and stock history in one case | Case file, evidence |
| Action plan | Store fixes tracked to closure | Action plan, due dates |

## Protection matches each item's risk
Layout: matrix
- Secure: High value, high theft - locked case or counter
- Protect: High value, low theft - tags and cameras
- Deter: Low value, high theft - tags and facing limits
- Monitor: Low value, low theft - cycle counts
> Notes: an example policy - place your own item groups in each box from shrink and sales data.

## Controls and exceptions
| Control | How it works | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| High-risk counts | Top-loss items counted weekly in every store | Inventory control |
| Refund controls | No-receipt refunds need ID and a manager | Store manager |
| Case standards | Every case has evidence, value and an outcome | Asset protection |
| ORC partnerships | Linked incidents shared with retailers and police | Asset protection lead |

## KPIs that show it works
| KPI | What it measures | Data source |
|---|---|---|
| Shrink rate | Loss as a share of sales | Inventory results |
| High-risk item loss | Units lost on the high-risk list | High-risk item counts |
| Exceptions worked | Alerts reviewed within [n] days | Case management |
| Recovery value | Value recovered from closed cases | Case outcomes |

## Today and target
### Today
- Shrink seen once a year at the full count
- Investigations start from a tip, not from data
### Target
- Weekly exception alerts by store and associate
- Protection set by each item's risk
