# Store labor scheduling

## What store labor scheduling covers
### Purpose
- The right people on the floor when guests come
- Spend the labor budget where it sells
### In scope
- Forecast, labor standards and budget
- Schedules, swaps, time and attendance
### Outcome
- Schedules published on time, within budget
- Coverage that matches traffic and tasks

## Hours follow the forecast, not habit
Layout: swimlane handoffs
- Labor planning: Sets the labor standards
- Workforce system: Forecasts sales, traffic and tasks
- Workforce system: Earns the week's hours
- Store manager: Reviews the draft schedule
- Store manager: Within budget and rules?
- Store manager: If yes, publish to the team
- Store manager: If no, rebalance the shifts
- Associate: Swaps shifts and clocks in

## Systems and data for labor scheduling
| Step | What the system does | Records it keeps |
|---|---|---|
| Forecast | Sales, traffic, deliveries and services by interval | Driver forecast by 15 minutes |
| Earned hours | Labor standards turn drivers into hours | Labor standards, hour budget |
| Schedule | Shifts matched to skills, availability and rules | Schedule, shift assignments |
| Time and pay | Punches checked against schedule and rules | Time records, payroll hours |

## Every week runs the same labor cycle
Layout: process cycle
- Forecast: drivers by 15 minutes
- Earn: hours from labor standards
- Schedule: shifts by skill and rule
- Publish: [n] weeks ahead
- Review: actual vs scheduled hours

## Controls and exceptions
| Control | How it works | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| Advance notice | Schedules published by the policy or legal deadline | Store manager |
| Break and overtime rules | Missed breaks and overtime flagged before publishing | Labor planning |
| Budget check | Scheduled vs earned hours reviewed weekly | District manager |
| Licensed roles | Salon services scheduled only to licensed staff | Salon manager |

## KPIs that show it works
| KPI | What it measures | Data source |
|---|---|---|
| Schedule vs earned hours | Scheduled hours against earned hours | Workforce system |
| Coverage fit | Staff on the floor vs traffic by hour | Schedule and door counts |
| Schedule changes | Shifts changed after publishing | Schedule history |
| Labor cost rate | Payroll as a share of sales | Payroll, sales |
