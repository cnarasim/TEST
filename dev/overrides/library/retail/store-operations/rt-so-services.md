# Salon and beauty services

## What salon and beauty services cover
### Purpose
- A great service, on time, every visit
- Keep chairs full and guests coming back
### In scope
- Booking, consultation, service and checkout
- Retail add-on, loyalty points and rebooking
### Outcome
- Higher chair utilization and rebooking
- Retail sales attached to every service

## Guests see the service, not the prep
Layout: swimlane blueprint
- Guest: Books online or by phone
- Salon team: Confirms the stylist and time
- Guest: Checks in at the salon
- Salon team: Consults and agrees the service
- Back bar: Preps tools, mixes color
- Salon team: Performs the service
- Guest: Pays, tips and rebooks
- Systems: Records sale and points
> Notes: a service blueprint - guest actions on top, the salon team in front of the guest, the back bar (prep area and professional products) out of sight, and the systems that support every step.

## Systems and data for salon services
| Step | What the system does | Records it keeps |
|---|---|---|
| Booking | Services, stylists and times offered online and by phone | Appointment, service menu |
| Consultation | The guest's history and notes shown to the stylist | Service history, formulas, notes |
| Checkout | Services, retail and tips paid at the POS | Service ticket, sale, tip |
| Loyalty and rebook | Points posted; the next visit booked or reminded | Points, next appointment |

## Rebooking keeps the chairs full
Layout: process cycle
- Book: online, in the app or by phone
- Consult: goals, history and a quote
- Serve: on time, to the standard
- Recommend: products to keep the look
- Rebook: the next visit at checkout

## Controls and exceptions
| Control | How it works | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| Licensed staff | Services booked only to licensed professionals | Salon manager |
| Allergy test | Color guests patch-tested first, per policy | Stylist |
| Sanitation log | Tools and stations cleaned and logged for each guest | Salon lead |
| No-show tracking | No-shows and short-notice cancels tracked by guest | Salon coordinator |

## KPIs that show it works
| KPI | What it measures | Data source |
|---|---|---|
| Chair utilization | Booked hours vs available hours | Booking system |
| Rebooking rate | Guests who book the next visit at checkout | Appointments |
| Retail attach | Retail sales per service ticket | POS service tickets |
| Service rating | Guest rating after the visit | Post-visit survey |

## Today and target
### Today
- Bookings by phone, notes on paper cards
- Stylists idle between walk-ins
### Target
- Online booking with the guest's history
- Rebooking and product tips at every checkout
