# Promotion planning and execution

## What this process covers
### Purpose
- Run promotions that grow sales and margin
- Have the stock in store when the offer starts
### In scope
- Offer design, promo price and forecast lift
- Stock build, activation and post-event review
### Outcome
- Promotions live on time in every channel
- A clear read on what each promotion earned

## Marketing plans, merchants price, supply chain builds stock
Layout: swimlane handoffs
- Marketing: Sets the promotion calendar
- Merchant: Chooses articles and offer
- Pricing analyst: Sets the promo price
- Demand planner: Forecasts the lift
- Merchant: Lift worth the margin?
- Merchant: If yes, activate the promotion
- Merchant: If no, change the offer
- Analyst: Reviews the results

## The systems and data it runs on
| Step | What the system does | Records it keeps |
|---|---|---|
| Promotion | Offer, items, channels and dates | Promotion record |
| Promo price | A temporary price or discount with an end date | Promotional price |
| Activation | Prices and offers sent to POS and the site | POS and site offer files |
| Stock build | Expected lift added to forecast or allocation | Lift, allocation |
| Results | Sales during vs before the event | Promotion results |

## Five steps take a promotion from idea to results
Layout: process numbered
- Plan: calendar and offer
- Price: promo price
- Stock: lift and allocation
- Activate: POS and online
- Review: lift and margin

## Controls and exceptions
| Control | How it works | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| Promotion approval | Offers below the margin floor need approval | Merchandising lead |
| Stock readiness | Stores stocked before the start date | Supply chain |
| Price end date | Every promo price has an end date | Pricing analyst |
| Post-event review | Every major promotion reviewed in two weeks | Analytics |

## KPIs that show it works
| KPI | What it measures | Data source |
|---|---|---|
| Promotion lift | Extra units sold vs baseline | Sales vs forecast baseline |
| Promo margin | Margin earned during the event | Margin by promotion |
| In-stock during promotion | Stores in stock while the offer runs | Store stock |
| Promo price accuracy | Registers charging the promo price | Store audits |

## Today and target
### Today
- Promotions planned in slides and e-mail
- Results read weeks later, if at all
### Target
- Every promotion in one system with price, stock and end date
- A standard post-event review
