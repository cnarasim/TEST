# Brand partner business review

## What the review covers
### Purpose
- One view of how the brand performs with us
- Agree what each side does next
### In scope
- Sales, margin and sell-through
- Supply, compliance and launches
### Outcome
- Joint actions with owners and dates
- Investments agreed for next quarter

## The quarter in four numbers
Layout: four numbers
- Sales: $18.4M (+6.2%)
- Margin: 41.5% (+0.8 pts)
- Fill rate: 93% (-2 pts)
- Sell-through: 68% of receipts
> Notes: illustrative figures - replace with the quarter's reporting.

## How the review runs
Layout: swimlane handoffs
- Merchant: Shares scorecard a week ahead
- Brand partner: Brings sell-in, plans and launches
- Planning: Confirms forecast and inventory
- Merchant: Targets met?
- Merchant: If yes, agrees growth actions
- Brand partner: If no, commits to a recovery plan
- Merchant: Records actions, owners and dates

## The review cycle
Layout: process cycle
- Prepare: scorecard and insights
- Review: results with the brand
- Agree: actions and investments
- Track: monthly check-ins
