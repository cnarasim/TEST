# Vendor scorecard and compliance

## What we measure
Layout: hexagons
- Fill rate: what we ordered, delivered
- On time: inside the delivery window
- ASN accuracy: the notice matches the cartons
- Labels: cartons labelled to standard
- Cost accuracy: invoice matches the agreed cost
- Response: questions answered in time

## The scorecard
| KPI | Weight | Target | Actual | Score |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Fill rate | 30% | 97% | 93% | 96 |
| On-time delivery | 25% | 95% | 94% | 99 |
| ASN accuracy | 20% | 98% | 99% | 100 |
| Label compliance | 15% | 99% | 97% | 98 |
| Cost accuracy | 10% | 99% | 99.5% | 100 |
| Total | 100% | | | 98 |
> Notes: illustrative - score is actual against target, capped at 100; the total is weighted.

## How compliance works
Layout: swimlane handoffs
- DC: Records ASN receipts
- Supply chain: Scores vendors monthly
- Supply chain: Below target?
- Merchant: If yes, agrees a fix plan
- Supply chain: If no, files the scorecard
- Vendor compliance: Charges repeat breaches
- Brand partner: Accepts or disputes

## Compliance rules and charges
| Rule | What it means | Charge |
|---|---|---|
| Late delivery | Outside the booked window | Per purchase order |
| Missing or wrong ASN | Cartons arrive without a matching notice | Per carton |
| Label errors | Carton label missing or unreadable | Per carton |
| Short shipment | Below the agreed fill rate | Per unit short |
> Notes: charges follow the vendor guide; amounts are set there, not on this slide.

## The improvement cycle
Layout: process cycle
- Measure: monthly, every vendor
- Share: scorecard in the vendor portal
- Agree: fixes and dates
- Improve: tracked at the business review
