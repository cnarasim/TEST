# New brand launch

## What a launch covers
### Purpose
- A new brand on shelf and online on one date
- Every team ready at the same time
### In scope
- Terms, vendor and item set-up, space, stock
- Marketing, content and store readiness
### Outcome
- Launch on the planned date
- First-month sales on target

## Who does what
Layout: swimlane handoffs
- Merchant: Selects brand, agrees terms
- Vendor set-up: Creates vendor and items
- Planning: Allocates first stock
- Planning: Adds the brand to planograms
- Marketing: Plans the launch campaign
- Merchant: Ready for launch?
- Stores: If yes, sets fixtures, opens sales
- Merchant: If no, moves launch, tells the brand

## Systems and data for a launch
| Step | What the system does | Records it keeps |
|---|---|---|
| Vendor set-up | One governed vendor record with terms | Vendor master, terms |
| Item set-up | Items with sizes, shades and barcodes | Item master, barcodes |
| Cost and price | Cost from the brand, retail price set | Cost agreement, price list |
| Space | Planograms for each store cluster | Planograms, store clusters |
| Content | Images and copy for the site and app | Product content |
| First order | Opening order and allocation to stores | Purchase order, allocation |

## From selection to first sale
Layout: process chevrons
- Select: brand and range
- Agree: terms and dates
- Set up: vendor, items, prices
- Ship: opening stock to DCs
- Launch: stores and online
- Review: first 30 days

## Launch checklist
| Item | Owner | Due | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Terms signed | Merchant | Week -12 | Done |
| Items and barcodes set up | Vendor set-up | Week -8 | Done |
| Planograms published | Space planning | Week -6 | On track |
| Opening stock in DCs | Planning | Week -3 | On track |
| Site and app content live | Marketing | Week -1 | At risk |

## Launch timeline
Layout: gantt
- Terms and set-up: Jan 2027 – Mar 2027 (Merchandising)
- Space and fixtures: Mar 2027 – Apr 2027 (Stores)
- Stock to DCs: Apr 2027 – May 2027 (Supply chain)
- Campaign: Apr 2027 – Jun 2027 (Marketing)
- Launch day: May 15, 2027 (Merchandising)

## KPIs that show it works
| KPI | What it measures | Data source |
|---|---|---|
| Launch on time | Launch date met in every channel | Launch plan |
| First-month sales | Sales against the launch plan | Sales reporting |
| In-stock at launch | Stores with the full range on day one | Store inventory |
| Content complete | Items with images and copy online | Product content |
