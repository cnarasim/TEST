# Brand shop-in-shop launch

## What a shop-in-shop launch covers
### Purpose
- A brand experience guests visit the store for
- Shared goals and costs agreed with the brand
### In scope
- Agreement, stores, fixtures and staffing
- Stock, launch events and results
### Outcome
- Higher sales per square foot in the space
- A repeatable play for the next stores

## Five phases from deal to review
Layout: process phases
- Agree: terms, funding and goals with the brand (T-20 weeks)
- Select: stores by sales, space and guest fit (T-16 weeks)
- Build: fixtures made and installed (T-6 weeks)
- Launch: staff trained, stock in place, event (T-0)
- Review: sales and guest results vs goals (T+12 weeks)

## The brand and Ulta share the work
Layout: swimlane handoffs
- Brand partner: Agrees terms and funds fixtures
- Merchandising: Selects the stores
- Store design: Plans the space and fixtures
- Store design: Fixtures approved by the brand?
- Store design: If yes, schedules the installs
- Store design: If no, revises the design
- Store operations: Trains staff and launches

## Launch checklist by area
| Area | Ready when | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| Agreement | Terms, funding and goals signed | Merchandising |
| Space | Planogram and fixtures installed | Store design |
| Stock | Launch stock and testers in store | Supply chain |
| People | Brand and store staff trained | Store operations |
| Launch event | Event and guest messages set | Marketing |

## How the space is judged
| KPI | What it measures | Data source |
|---|---|---|
| Sales per square foot | Space productivity vs before | POS and space data |
| Brand sales lift | Brand sales vs similar stores | POS |
| New guests | First-time buyers of the brand | Loyalty data |
| Launch on time | Stores opened on the planned date | Project plan |

## Today and target
### Today
- Each brand space planned from scratch
- Results judged on brand sales alone
### Target
- One play for every shop-in-shop
- Space judged on total store results
