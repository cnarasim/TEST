# Joint business plan

## Our shared ambition
Layout: four numbers
- Sales: $80M next year (+9%)
- Launches: 120 new items
- New guests: 25% of the brand's buyers new to it
- Margin: 41% (+1 pt)
> Notes: illustrative targets - agreed with the brand at the start of the year.

## Where growth comes from
Layout: matrix
- Assortment: launches and exclusives guests come for
- Space: more shelf in the top stores
- Marketing: member offers, events and content
- Digital: app and site that convert

## How we build the plan together
Layout: swimlane handoffs
- Merchant: Shares the category strategy
- Brand partner: Shares plan and launches
- Planning: Forecasts sales and inventory
- Marketing: Plans campaigns and events
- Merchant: Plan meets both sides' targets?
- Merchant: If yes, signs the plan together
- Brand partner: If no, revises investments and terms

## The plan by quarter
Layout: timeline quarters
- Spring launch: Feb 2027 – Apr 2027 (Assortment)
- Member event: May 2027 – May 2027 (Marketing)
- Space reset: Jul 2027 – Aug 2027 (Stores)
- Holiday gift sets: Oct 2027 – Dec 2027 (Assortment)
> Notes: example dates.

## Investments and who funds them
| Lever | Investment | Who funds | Expected return |
|---|---|---|---|
| Exclusive launch | $1.2M | Brand partner | $6M sales |
| Member event | $0.4M | Shared 50/50 | 40K new buyers |
| Space reset | $0.3M | Retailer | +8% sales in reset stores |
| Digital content | $0.2M | Brand partner | +0.5 pts conversion |

## Five planning steps
Layout: process chevrons
- Align: ambition and targets
- Plan: levers and calendar
- Fund: investments and terms
- Execute: launches and events
- Review: quarterly business review

## How we measure the plan
| KPI | Baseline | Target | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|
| Brand sales | $73M | $80M | Merchant |
| New buyers | 20% | 25% | Marketing |
| Fill rate | 94% | 97% | Brand partner |
| Launches on time | 85% | 100% | Brand partner |

## Today and target
### Today
- Plans agreed separately with each team
- Funding tracked in spreadsheets
### Target
- One plan signed by both sides
- Funding and returns tracked quarterly
