# Merchandise financial planning and OTB

## What this process covers
### Purpose
- Set sales, margin and stock targets per category
- Control buying with open-to-buy
### In scope
- Top-down and bottom-up plans by month
- Open-to-buy, in-season re-forecast
### Outcome
- An approved plan every buyer works to
- Purchases inside the budget, visible weekly

## Finance sets the frame, merchants fill it
Layout: swimlane handoffs
- Finance: Sets the top-down sales and margin target
- Merch planner: Builds the category plan by month
- Merch planner: Plan meets the target?
- Merch planner: If yes, submit for sign-off
- Merch planner: If no, rebalance categories
- Merchant: Buys within open-to-buy
- Merch planner: Re-forecasts in season

## The systems and data it runs on
| Step | What the system does | Records it keeps |
|---|---|---|
| Plan | Top-down and bottom-up plans by category and month | Plan versions |
| Actuals | Sales, margin and stock flow in from the ERP | Actual sales and stock |
| Open-to-buy | Each purchase is checked against the budget | Open-to-buy by category |
| Purchases | Buyers commit the plan on purchase orders | Purchase orders |
| Re-forecast | In-season versions with the latest trend | Plan vs actual |

## Controls and exceptions
| Control | How it works | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| Plan sign-off | Category plans approved by finance and merchandising | Merch planning lead |
| OTB check | A PO beyond open-to-buy warns or blocks | Merch planner |
| Version control | One approved version per season | Finance |
| Weekly variance | Sales and stock vs plan reviewed every week | Merch planner |

## KPIs that show it works
| KPI | What it measures | Data source |
|---|---|---|
| Sales vs plan | Actual sales against the approved plan | Plan vs actual |
| Gross margin rate | Margin as a share of sales | Ledger |
| GMROI | Margin earned per dollar of average stock | Margin and stock value |
| OTB utilisation | Budget committed on orders | Open-to-buy vs open orders |
