# New item setup and launch

## What a new item launch covers
### Purpose
- Launch new items on the date the brand promotes
- Sell from day one, in store and online
### In scope
- Item data, compliance, cost and price
- Assortment, space, first order and content
### Outcome
- Items set up complete, the first time
- In stock on the shelf and online at launch

## The brand submits, we check, then buy
Layout: swimlane handoffs
- Brand partner: Submits the item and data
- Item setup team: Checks data and compliance
- Item setup team: Complete and compliant?
- Item setup team: If yes, activate the item
- Brand partner: If no, correct and resubmit
- Merchant: Sets price, stores and buy
- Supply chain: Receives and allocates by date

## Systems and data for a new item
| Step | What the system does | Records it keeps |
|---|---|---|
| Submission | The brand enters item data in the supplier portal | Item request, attributes, images |
| Compliance | Ingredients, claims and hazmat data checked | Ingredient list, safety data sheet |
| Item and price | Item created with barcode, cost and retail | Item master, cost, retail price |
| Buy and allocate | First order placed and split to DCs and stores | Purchase order, allocation |

## Every launch counts back from one date
Layout: timeline countdown
- Item data in: Feb 1, 2027 (Brand partner)
- Item approved: Feb 22, 2027 (Item setup)
- First order placed: Mar 1, 2027 (Merchant)
- Stock in the DC: Apr 12, 2027 (Supply chain)
- Launch day: May 3, 2027 (Merchandising)
> Notes: example dates - count back from your own launch date and lead times.

## Controls and exceptions
| Control | How it works | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| Mandatory data | No activation without barcode, cost, retail and ingredients | Item setup team |
| Compliance review | Claims, warnings and hazmat checked before the buy | Product compliance |
| Street date | Items blocked at the POS until the launch date | Merchandising |
| Launch readiness | Stock, space and content checked a week out | Merchant |

## KPIs that show it works
| KPI | What it measures | Data source |
|---|---|---|
| Setup cycle time | Submission to active item, in days | Item request dates |
| First-time-right | Submissions approved without rework | Item request history |
| Launch on time | Items selling on the launch date | First sale vs launch date |
| Launch in-stock | Stores and site in stock on launch day | Store and DC inventory |
