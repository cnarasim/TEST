# Product content and digital shelf

## What product content covers
### Purpose
- Help guests choose online as well as in store
- Be found when guests search by need
### In scope
- Images, copy, ingredients, shades, claims
- Attributes, filters, reviews and search
### Outcome
- Every item live with complete content
- More conversion, fewer returns

## Brands supply, our team enriches
Layout: swimlane handoffs
- Brand partner: Sends images, copy and ingredients
- PIM: Checks completeness by rule
- Content team: Enriches attributes and filters
- Content team: Claims and copy compliant?
- Content team: If yes, publish to site and app
- Brand partner: If no, revise the content
- Ecommerce team: Monitors reviews and search

## Systems and data for product content
| Step | What the system does | Records it keeps |
|---|---|---|
| Intake | The PIM takes brand content by feed or portal | Product content, images |
| Enrich | Shade, skin type, concern and finish added | Attributes, taxonomy |
| Publish | Site, app and search get the approved content | Published content, version |
| Measure | Content scores and reviews tracked by item | Content score, ratings |

## Six things make a complete product page
Layout: process list
- Images: front, back, swatch and texture
- Copy: benefits and how to use
- Ingredients: the full list, checked
- Attributes: shade, skin type, concern
- Claims: approved wording only
- Reviews: ratings from real guests

## Controls and exceptions
| Control | How it works | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| Go-live gate | No item online without images, copy and ingredients | Ecommerce team |
| Claims review | Claims checked against the approved list | Product compliance |
| Shade accuracy | Swatches checked against the product | Content team |
| Broken content scan | Missing images and dead links found daily | Site operations |

## KPIs that show it works
| KPI | What it measures | Data source |
|---|---|---|
| Content score | Items with complete content, weighted | PIM content checks |
| Time to live | Item active to content published | PIM and site dates |
| Conversion rate | Product page views that end in a sale | Site analytics |
| Search findability | Items found for their key search terms | Search reports |

## Today and target
### Today
- Content copied from brand sites, gaps unseen
- Filters miss shade and skin type
### Target
- One PIM feeding the site, the app and search
- A content score that gates go-live
