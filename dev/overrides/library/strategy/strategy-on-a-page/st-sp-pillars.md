# Strategic pillars and enablers

## Three pillars carry the strategy
### Grow
- More guests, more often
- New categories and services
### Delight
- One experience across store and app
- Faster pickup and delivery
### Simplify
- Fewer, better processes
- Lower cost to serve

## Six enablers make the pillars possible
Layout: process list
- Data: One guest and product view
- Platforms: one ERP core for every team
- Supply chain: Stock where guests want it
- People: Skills for the new ways of working
- Governance: Clear decisions and owners
- Partners: Brands and suppliers as one team

## Pillar roadmap
Layout: timeline quarters
- Guest profile live: Jan 2027 – Mar 2027 (Grow)
- App pickup redesign: Feb 2027 – May 2027 (Delight)
- Automatic replenishment: Mar 2027 – Jul 2027 (Simplify)
- New services pilot: Jun 2027 – Sep 2027 (Grow)
- Peak readiness: Oct 1, 2027 (Simplify)
