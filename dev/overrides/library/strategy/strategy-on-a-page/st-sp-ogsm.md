# OGSM

## Strategy on a page
Layout: OGSM
- Objective: Become the first choice for beauty in every channel
- Goal: Grow comparable sales every year
- Goal: Lift member repeat purchase
- Goal: Raise operating margin
- Strategy: Guest experience — one profile, one promise in store and online
- Strategy: Assortment — the brands and newness guests come for
- Strategy: Operations — the right stock, at lower cost per unit
- Measure: Repeat rate — [target] — buy again in 90 days
- Measure: Newness share — [target] — sales from new items
- Measure: Cost per unit — [target] — supply cost per unit
> Notes: OGSM keeps one objective, three goals, three strategies and three measures on the template slide; replace the [target] values with your own.

## Initiatives behind each strategy
| Strategy | Initiatives | Owner | Measure |
|---|---|---|---|
| Guest experience | Unified guest profile; app pickup flow | Digital | Repeat rate |
| Assortment | Newness pipeline; store-cluster ranges | Merchandising | Newness share |
| Operations | Automatic replenishment; DC automation | Supply chain | Cost per unit |

## How we track it
| Measure | Cadence | Forum | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Repeat rate | Monthly | Executive review | Loyalty data |
| Newness share | Monthly | Merchandising review | Sales by launch date |
| Cost per unit | Monthly | Operations review | Supply chain cost and volume |
