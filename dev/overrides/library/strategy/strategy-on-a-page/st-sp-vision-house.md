# Vision and strategic priorities

## Our priorities
Layout: priorities house
- Vision: The beauty destination guests choose first, in store and online
- Goal: Outgrow the beauty market
- Goal: Lift margin every year
- Goal: Most loved beauty retailer
- Strategy: Guest first — one profile and one promise in every channel (Repeat rate; Satisfaction)
- Strategy: Winning assortment — the brands and newness guests come for (Sales per foot; Newness share)
- Strategy: Stores as hubs — services, pickup and ship-from-store (Store sales; Pickup share)
- Strategy: Digital growth — app and site that convert (Conversion; App sales)
- Strategy: Efficient supply chain — the right stock in the right place (In-stock; Cost per unit)
- Strategy: Great teams — skilled, engaged associates (Engagement; Retention)
> Notes: replace the vision, goals and strategies with your own; keep three goals and six strategies so the house stays balanced.

## Where we play
### Guests
- Beauty enthusiasts and new-to-beauty guests

### Categories
- Cosmetics, skincare, fragrance, haircare
- Services that bring guests back
### Channels
- Stores, app and site as one experience
- Pickup and same-day delivery
