# Scenario planning

Plan for the futures that could happen, not only the one we hope for.

## Two uncertainties matter most
Layout: process list
| Driver | What could happen | Certainty | Impact |
|---|---|---|---|
| Guest spending | Strong or cautious | Low | High |
| Channel shift | Online grows fast or slowly | Low | High |
| Costs and tariffs | Rising or stable | Medium | Medium |
| Ageing population | Older guests, more wellness | High | Medium |
| Ingredient rules | Tighter rules on claims | High | Medium |
> Notes: example drivers; the two with low certainty and high impact become the axes of the four scenarios.

## Four futures on one page
Layout: matrix
- Digital boom: strong spending, fast shift online
- Store revival: strong spending, stores stay central
- Value hunt: cautious spending, fast shift online
- Steady squeeze: cautious spending, stores stay central

## What each future means for us
Layout: process list
| Scenario | What it means | Our best move |
|---|---|---|
| Digital boom | Online wins most growth | Scale the app and same-day |
| Store revival | Visits and services grow | Invest in stores and services |
| Value hunt | Guests trade down | Value tiers and member offers |
| Steady squeeze | Flat sales, cost pressure | Cut the cost to serve |

## No-regret moves pay off in every future
### No-regret moves
- Pay off in every scenario
- One guest profile, flexible fulfillment
### Options
- Small bets we scale if a future arrives
- Smaller formats, new services
### Hedges
- Protect against the worst case
- Flexible costs and short contracts

## Signposts show which future is arriving
| Signpost | What we watch | Points to |
|---|---|---|
| Online share of beauty | Monthly market data | Digital boom or value hunt |
| Basket size and trips | Our sales data | Strong or cautious spending |
| Store traffic | Visits against last year | Store revival or squeeze |
| Discount depth | Competitor promotions | Value hunt |

## How we keep the scenarios alive
Layout: process cycle
- Scan: drivers and signposts
- Test: the strategy in each future
- Act: no-regret moves and options
- Review: every six months

## Fund the no-regret moves now
- Decision: Fund the no-regret moves in this year's plan
- Ask: Agree the signposts and the trigger for each option
- Next step: Review the scenarios in six months
