# Industry forces analysis

## Five forces shape the beauty market
Layout: hub
- Rivalry: specialists, mass and online chase one guest
- New entrants: brand-direct and social commerce
- Substitutes: salons, clinics and at-home devices
- Supplier power: prestige brands choose their retailers
- Buyer power: guests compare prices in seconds

## How strong each force is and its trend
| Force | What drives it | Strength | Trend |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rivalry | Many specialists, mass and online | High | Rising |
| New entrants | Low cost to launch a brand online | Medium | Rising |
| Substitutes | Salons, clinics, at-home devices | Medium | Steady |
| Supplier power | Prestige brands pick their retailers | High | Steady |
| Buyer power | Easy price checks, no switching cost | High | Rising |
> Notes: example ratings; agree the strength and trend of each force with evidence before you use them.

## Strong, rising forces come first
Layout: matrix
- Act now: strong and rising - rivalry, buyer power
- Defend: strong and steady - supplier power
- Watch: weaker but rising - new entrants
- Monitor: weaker and steady - substitutes

## Our response to each force
| Force | Our response | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| Rivalry | Win on services and expertise | Stores |
| New entrants | Be the launch partner for brands | Brand partnerships |
| Substitutes | Add services and wellness | Services |
| Supplier power | Joint plans and exclusives | Merchandising |
| Buyer power | Loyalty value, price confidence | Marketing |

## How we run the analysis each year
Layout: process numbered
- Define: the market and its edges
- Gather: evidence for each force
- Rate: strength and trend
- Respond: moves for the strongest forces
- Refresh: once a year, or after a shock

## So what for the strategy
- Decision: Focus on rivalry and buyer power this year
- Ask: Owners bring a plan for each response
- Next step: Refresh the analysis every year
