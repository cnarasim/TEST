# External scan

## Six forces shaping the market
Layout: process list
- Political: trade and tariff changes
- Economic: cautious spending, value seeking
- Social: wellness and self-care routines
- Technology: AI-led discovery and personalisation
- Legal: ingredient and data rules tighten
- Environmental: refill, packaging and transparency

## Impact and readiness
| Force | What changes | Impact on us | How ready we are |
|---|---|---|---|
| Economic | Guests trade down in some categories | High | Medium |
| Social | Wellness joins beauty | High | High |
| Technology | Discovery moves to AI and video | High | Medium |
| Legal | New ingredient disclosure rules | Medium | Low |
| Environmental | Packaging rules and refills | Medium | Medium |

## Where to act first
Layout: matrix
- Act now: high impact, low readiness - ingredient rules, AI discovery
- Build: high impact, medium readiness - value tiers, refills
- Watch: lower impact, low readiness - tariff scenarios
- Maintain: lower impact, high readiness - wellness assortment

## So what for the strategy
- Decision: Fund ingredient transparency and AI discovery this year
- Ask: Legal and merchandising own the disclosure plan
- Next step: Refresh the scan every six months
