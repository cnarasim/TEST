# Competitive landscape

## Where competitors play
Layout: matrix
- Prestige specialists: high price, narrow range - expertise and brands
- Mass retailers: low price, broad range - convenience and value
- Online pure players: wide range, fast delivery - choice and speed
- Brand direct: one brand, deep content - community and newness
