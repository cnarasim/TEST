# Growth options

## Four ways to grow
Layout: matrix
- Market penetration: existing products, existing guests - more visits and spend
- Product development: new products, existing guests - new categories and services
- Market development: existing products, new guests or places - new regions and formats
- Diversification: new products, new markets - new businesses

## Options in each
Layout: process list
| Path | Option | Size of prize | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| Penetration | Lift member frequency | High | Low |
| Product development | Wellness category | Medium | Medium |
| Market development | Smaller-format stores | Medium | Medium |
| Diversification | Professional salon supply | Low | High |
