# Three horizons

## Three horizons on one timeline
Layout: timeline phases
- H1 core: Jan 2027 to Dec 2027 (Defend and extend today's business)
- H2 emerging: Jan 2027 to Dec 2028 (Grow businesses that are starting)
- H3 future: Jan 2028 to Dec 2030 (Create options for the long term)

## What sits in each horizon
| Horizon | Initiatives | Measure of success | Share of investment |
|---|---|---|---|
| Core | Stores, loyalty, replenishment | Sales, margin | 70% |
| Emerging | Services, wellness, retail media | Growth rate | 20% |
| Future | New formats, new markets | Options proven | 10% |

## How we fund the horizons
Layout: donut
- Core: 70% of spend
- Emerging: 20% of spend
- Future: 10% of spend

## How we manage each
### Core
- Annual budgets, efficiency targets
### Emerging
- Stage funding against growth milestones
### Future
- Small bets, fast learning, kill quickly
