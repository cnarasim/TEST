# Annual operating plan

## Three priorities shape next year
### Grow
- Sales growth ahead of the beauty market
- New brands, categories and services
### Serve
- Pickup and delivery guests can count on
- Stock and staff ready for every peak
### Simplify
- Lower cost to serve every order
- Fewer, simpler processes and systems

## The risks that could move the plan
| Risk | Impact | Mitigation | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|
| Weaker guest spending | Sales below plan | Flex costs by quarter | Finance |
| Tariff and cost increases | Lower margin | Price and sourcing plans | Merchandising |
| Initiatives delivered late | Benefits slip | Monthly initiative review | Transformation office |
| Peak capacity | Lost sales at peak | Peak readiness plan | Operations |
