# Digital strategy

## Three pillars carry the digital ambition
### Discover
- Search and content that know each guest
- Tutorials, reviews and shoppable video
### Buy
- Fast checkout in the app and on the site
- Pickup, same-day and ship from store
### Belong
- Loyalty and rewards in every visit
- Services booked and remembered

## The guest journeys we will win
| Journey | Pain today | Digital fix |
|---|---|---|
| Find the right product | Search misses what guests mean | Smarter search, shade matching |
| Buy and collect | Order status unclear | Order status and pickup slots |
| Book a service | Booking apart from shopping | One account for both |
| Reorder a favorite | Bought elsewhere by habit | One-tap reorder, reminders |
| Use rewards | Points hard to see | Rewards shown at every step |

## Six capabilities make digital work
Layout: hexagons
- Guest data: one profile across channels
- Personalization: the right offer and content
- Commerce platform: fast and easy to change
- Content: tutorials, reviews, shoppable video
- Inventory: store and DC stock visible online
- Experimentation: test and learn every week

## Where digital investment goes first
Layout: matrix
- Quick wins: high value, low effort - live order status
- Big bets: high value, high effort - guest profile, new search
- Fill-ins: lower value, low effort - saved routines
- Not now: lower value, high effort - separate brand apps
> Notes: example placements; score each initiative on value and effort before placing it.

## The digital roadmap by quarter
Layout: timeline quarters
- Unified guest profile: Jan 2027 to Mar 2027 (Data)
- New search and discovery: Feb 2027 to Jun 2027 (Digital)
- Live order status: Apr 2027 to Jul 2027 (Fulfillment)
- Services in the app: Jun 2027 to Oct 2027 (Services)
- Peak readiness: Oct 1, 2027 (Digital)
