# Omnichannel strategy

## One guest, one promise, every channel
Layout: process list
- Guest: one profile, one promise
- Stores: advice, services and pickup
- Site: the full range, any time
- App: loyalty, reorder, live status
- Customer care: help in any channel
- Social: inspiration linked to stock

## Today and target
### Today
- Channels run as separate businesses
- Store stock is invisible online
### Target
- One guest, one inventory, one promise
- Every store works as a fulfillment hub

## Every channel has a clear job
| Channel | Role for the guest | What we measure |
|---|---|---|
| Stores | Try, get advice, pick up | Sales, pickup on time |
| Site | Browse the full range | Conversion, traffic |
| App | Loyalty, reorder, order status | Active members, app sales |
| Customer care | Fix problems in any channel | First contact resolution |

## Four ways an order reaches the guest

## Six enablers make one experience real
Layout: process list
- Inventory: store and DC stock visible online
- Order management: one brain for every order
- Guest profile: one view in every channel
- Store tools: associates see orders and guests
- Pricing: one price and promotion everywhere
- Returns: return anything, anywhere

## The omnichannel roadmap by quarter
Layout: timeline quarters
- Store stock on the site: Jan 2027 to Mar 2027 (Digital)
- Pickup slots and live status: Feb 2027 to May 2027 (Stores)
- Ship from store, wave 1: Apr 2027 to Aug 2027 (Supply chain)
- One returns flow: Jun 2027 to Sep 2027 (Stores)
- Peak readiness: Oct 1, 2027 (Omnichannel)
