# Business capability map and heatmap

## Capabilities build on each other
Layout: pyramid
- Differentiating: what wins customers
- Core: what runs the business every day
- Supporting: what keeps the lights on
- Foundation: data, integration, security

## Capabilities and the applications behind them
| Capability | Applications | Health | Investment |
|---|---|---|---|
| Merchandise planning | Planning tool | Good | Invest |
| Pricing, promotions | Pricing engine, spreadsheets | Poor | Migrate |
| Order management | Order management system | Good | Invest |
| Warehousing | WMS | Medium | Tolerate |
| Finance and close | ERP finance | Good | Tolerate |
| Customer service | Two overlapping tools | Poor | Consolidate |

## Where to invest
Layout: matrix
- Invest: strategic and healthy
- Fix: strategic but poor health
- Maintain: routine and healthy
- Simplify: routine and poor - consolidate

## Reading the heatmap
### Healthy
- One application, well supported
- Meets today's and next year's needs
### Watch
- Workarounds or ageing technology
- Plan a review within a year
### At risk
- Overlap, unsupported or failing
- On the next investment plan
