# Risks, assumptions, issues and dependencies

## Four things we track
Layout: matrix
- Risks: what might happen - owned, with a mitigation
- Assumptions: what we believe is true - checked by a date
- Issues: what has happened - owned, with an action
- Dependencies: what we need from others - agreed dates

## Top risks
| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Key users not available for testing | Medium | High | Backfill agreed with managers | Business lead |
| Data quality delays migration | High | High | Cleansing starts now, three trial loads | Data lead |
| Supplier release slips | Medium | Medium | Contract milestone with credits | Vendor manager |

## Assumptions and dependencies
Layout: process list
- Single sign-on: the identity team supports the new app (Assumption · security lead, by design sign-off)
- Integration capacity: new interfaces fit the current licence (Dependency · integration lead, by build start)
- Close calendar: no go-live in the finance close week (Dependency · programme manager, by plan baseline)

## The weekly rhythm
Layout: process cycle
- Identify: new items from every team
- Assess: likelihood, impact, urgency
- Act: owners and dates
- Review: weekly with the programme board
