# Comparative evaluation of applications

## How we compare
Layout: process numbered
- Criteria: agree what matters
- Weights: share 100 points
- Evidence: demos, references, a proof of concept
- Score: 1 to 5 per criterion
- Decide: totals and risks together

## Criteria and weights
Layout: process list
| Criterion | Weight | What good looks like |
|---|---|---|
| Functional fit | 30% | Most needs met as standard |
| Technical fit | 20% | Fits our architecture standards |
| Cost of ownership | 20% | Lowest three-year cost for the scope |
| Supplier strength | 15% | Stable, investing, good references |
| Delivery risk | 15% | Proven at our scale, short time to value |

## Weighted scores
| Criterion | Weight | Option A | Option B | Option C |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Functional fit | 30% | 4.5 | 4.0 | 3.0 |
| Technical fit | 20% | 4.0 | 3.5 | 4.0 |
| Cost of ownership | 20% | 3.5 | 4.0 | 3.0 |
| Supplier strength | 15% | 4.5 | 3.0 | 3.5 |
| Delivery risk | 15% | 4.0 | 3.0 | 2.5 |
| Weighted total | 100% | 4.1 | 3.6 | 3.2 |
> Notes: illustrative scores - replace with the evaluation team's scores; keep the method and the weights agreed up front.

## Option A leads on the weighted total
Layout: ranking
- Option A: 4.1
- Option B: 3.6
- Option C: 3.2

## Strengths and watch-outs
### Option A
- Strongest functional fit and references
- Watch: implementation partner capacity
### Option B
- Lowest run cost
- Watch: thinner supplier support
### Option C
- Best technical fit
- Watch: most custom work

## Recommendation
### Option A
- Highest weighted score, lowest delivery risk
- Negotiate the run cost down
### Option B
- Keep as the fallback
- Re-engage if negotiations fail

## The decision
- Decision: Select Option A, subject to contract terms
- Ask: Approve the budget for the proof of concept
- Next step: Proof of concept in six weeks
