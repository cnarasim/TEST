# Build, buy or reuse

## The right answer depends on the capability
Layout: matrix
- Reuse: we already own it - extend what works
- Buy: a commodity capability - adopt a standard product
- Build: what makes us different - own the code
- Partner: specialist and changing fast - a managed service

## Factor by factor
| Factor | Reuse | Buy | Build |
|---|---|---|---|
| Time to value | Fastest | Fast | Slowest |
| Cost to run | Lowest | Subscription | Team and platform |
| Differentiation | Low | Low | High |
| Control of the roadmap | Shared | Supplier's | Ours |
| Delivery risk | Low | Medium | High |

## One need, three paths
Layout: process diverge
- Need: a capability the business asks for
- Reuse: an application we own
- Buy: a standard product
- Build: our own software

## Our sourcing principles
Layout: process list
- Reuse first: look in the portfolio before the market (extend before adding a new tool)
- Buy the standard: commodity processes on standard products (change the process, not the product)
- Build to differentiate: only where we win customers (owned by a product team)

## The decision
- Decision: Buy a standard product for this capability
- Ask: Confirm no existing application covers it
- Next step: Start the vendor evaluation
