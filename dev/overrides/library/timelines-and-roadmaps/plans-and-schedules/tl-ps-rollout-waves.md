# Rollout waves: stores and DCs

## Waves on the timeline
Layout: gantt
- Pilot: Feb 2027 to Mar 2027 (Stores)
- Wave 1: Apr 2027 to May 2027 (Stores)
- Wave 2: Jun 2027 to Jul 2027 (Stores)
- Wave 3: Aug 2027 to Sep 2027 (Stores)
- DC 1: Mar 2027 to Mar 2027 (DCs)
- DCs 2 and 3: May 2027 to Jun 2027 (DCs)
> Notes: example dates; each DC goes live before the stores it serves, and nothing moves in the holiday freeze.

## How sites are grouped into waves
Layout: process list
- Region: districts together, one support team
- DC: stores follow the DC that serves them
- Size: smaller, simpler stores first
- Readiness: network, devices and staffing ready
- Calendar: no remodel or event that week
- Learning: each wave tests something new

## Waves grow as confidence grows
| Wave | Sites | Starts | Go on when |

| Pilot | 5 stores, 1 DC | [date] | Pilot exit criteria met |
| Wave 1 | 50 stores | [date] | Pilot issues fixed |

> Notes: illustrative wave sizes - set them from the support team's capacity.

## Every wave runs the same play
Layout: process phases
- Prepare: readiness check; training (T-4 weeks)
- Switch: overnight cutover (T-0)
- Support: floor walkers; daily calls (Week 1)
- Stabilise: fixes; lessons (Weeks 2-3)

## Stores and DCs move together
Layout: process converge
- DC live: receives and ships on the new system
- Store devices: in place and tested
- Store teams: trained in the last three weeks
- Result: The DC's stores go live - one flow, end to end

## Go / no-go for each wave
| Check | We go on if | Decides |
|---|---|---|
| Previous wave | No open critical issues | Rollout lead |
| Readiness | Every site in the wave rated ready | Regional leaders |
| Support | Floor walkers booked, service desk staffed | Support lead |
| Calendar | No freeze, event or remodel that week | Release manager |

## Rollout progress
Layout: rings
- Stores live: 38%
- DCs live: 50%
- Associates trained: 61%
> Notes: illustrative progress after wave 1.

## Lessons move from wave to wave
Layout: process cycle
- Run: the wave to the play
- Review: issues and tickets on day five
- Fix: playbook, training, system
- Apply: in the next wave
