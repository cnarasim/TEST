# Retail peak and event calendar

## The selling year at a glance
Layout: timeline quarters
- Spring beauty event: Mar 2027 to Apr 2027 (Marketing)
- Gifting for Mother's Day: Apr 2027 to May 2027 (Merchandising)
- Fall beauty event: Sep 2027 to Oct 2027 (Marketing)
- Holiday peak: Nov 2027 to Dec 2027 (All teams)
- Holiday change freeze: Nov 1, 2027 (Technology)
> Notes: example dates; replace them with this year's event calendar.

## Key dates every team plans around
Layout: timeline calendar
- Event plans locked: Jan 15, 2027 (Merchandising)
- Spring event starts: Mar 7, 2027 (Marketing)
- Peak readiness review: Sep 1, 2027 (Operations)
- Holiday freeze starts: Nov 1, 2027 (Technology)
- Peak weekend: Nov 26, 2027 (All teams)
> Notes: example dates.

## What each team must have ready
| Team | Ready by | What ready means |
|---|---|---|
| Merchandising | 12 weeks before | Offers, items and gift sets set |
| Supply chain | 2 weeks before | Event stock in DCs and stores |
| Stores | 1 week before | Sets, signs and staffing in place |
| Digital | 2 weeks before | Pages, offers and load test done |
| Technology | Freeze start | Changes done, support rota set |

## Freeze rules during peaks
| Rule | How it works | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| Change freeze | Only emergency changes in peak weeks | Change manager |
| No new launches | Projects avoid peak go-lives | Program office |
| Extra support | Longer support hours in peak | Service desk |
| Daily peak call | Sales, stock and issues reviewed | Operations lead |

- Go-lives land in the middle of events
### Target
