# Customer journey

## The journey
Layout: timeline journey
- Discover: Jan 2027 – Jan 2027 (Social, search, friends)
- Explore: Feb 2027 – Feb 2027 (App, site, store)
- Buy: Mar 2027 – Mar 2027 (Checkout, pickup, delivery)
- Use: Apr 2027 – Apr 2027 (Routine, tutorials)
- Return: May 2027 – May 2027 (Reorder, rewards, events)
> Notes: the months only order the stages; read each as a stage, not a date.

## What the guest does, thinks and feels
| Stage | Does | Thinks | Feels |
|---|---|---|---|
| Discover | Sees a product in a video | Would this work for me? | Curious |
| Explore | Compares shades and reviews | Which one is right? | Unsure |
| Buy | Orders for pickup | Will it be ready? | Hopeful |
| Use | Tries the routine | Is this working? | Pleased or let down |
| Return | Reorders with points | They know me | Loyal |

## Pain points and moments that matter
Layout: matrix
- Shade finding: unsure online - virtual try-on and shade match
- Pickup wait: unclear when ready - live status and slots
- First use: no guidance - routine tips after purchase
- Reorder: too many steps - one-tap reorder

## What we will improve first
- Decision: Shade match and pickup status first
- Ask: Digital and stores share one journey owner
- Next step: Measure satisfaction at each stage monthly
