# Multi-year roadmap

## Three years on one page
Layout: timeline phases
- Foundations: Jan 2027 – Dec 2027 (Year 1)
- Scale: Jan 2028 – Dec 2028 (Year 2)
- Lead: Jan 2029 – Dec 2029 (Year 3)
- Data platform live: Jun 30, 2027 (Year 1)
- Every store on the new model: Dec 31, 2028 (Year 2)

## The big moves, year by year
Layout: cards
- Year 1, foundations: data, people and core systems
- Year 2, scale: roll out to every store and channel
- Year 3, lead: new services and new markets

## Capabilities each year needs
| Capability | Year 1 | Year 2 | Year 3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Guest data | One profile | Real-time offers | Predictive service |
| Supply chain | Store replenishment | DC automation | Same-day network |
| Stores | Pickup and services | New format pilots | Format roll-out |
| People | Skills plan | Academy | Career paths |

## Milestones that matter
Layout: timeline vertical
- Data platform live: Jun 30, 2027 (Data)
- First automated DC: Mar 31, 2028 (Supply chain)
- Every store on the new model: Dec 31, 2028 (Stores)
- New services in every region: Sep 30, 2029 (Services)

## What has to be true
- Funding: capital released year by year against results
- People: the skills hired or built before each year starts
- Governance: one portfolio board, quarterly reviews
