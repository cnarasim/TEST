# Capability roadmap

## Capabilities by horizon
Layout: timeline now-next-later
- Unified guest profile: Jan 2027 to Mar 2027 (Now)
- Store pickup scheduling: Feb 2027 to May 2027 (Now)
- Automated store replenishment: Apr 2027 to Sep 2027 (Next)
- Services booking in the app: Jun 2027 to Oct 2027 (Next)
- DC automation phase 1: Jan 2028 to Jun 2028 (Later)
> Notes: example items and dates; Now is funded and staffed, Next is planned, Later is the idea list - items move left as they are funded.

## Each capability has an outcome and owner
| Capability | Outcome | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| Unified guest profile | One view of the guest in every channel | Marketing |
| Pickup scheduling | Orders ready when guests arrive | Store operations |
| Store replenishment | Fewer out-of-stocks on top sellers | Supply chain |
| Services booking | More bookings from the app | Services |
| DC automation | Lower cost per unit shipped | DC operations |

## Where investment goes
Layout: matrix
- Build now: high value, foundation ready - fund this year
- Prepare: high value, foundation missing - fix data first
- Improve: lower value, quick - small steps in current teams
- Hold: lower value, costly - revisit next planning cycle

## How the roadmap is managed
| Rule | How it works | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| Quarterly review | Horizons and priorities reset each quarter | Portfolio lead |
| Outcome first | Each item names the outcome it delivers | Capability owner |
| Funded means now | Only funded and staffed items sit in Now | Finance partner |
| One roadmap | Teams plan from this version | Enterprise architecture |
