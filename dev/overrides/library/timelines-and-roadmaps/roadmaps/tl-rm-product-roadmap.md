# Product roadmap

## Themes for the year
Layout: cards
- Find: guests find the right product faster
- Buy: checkout and pickup without friction
- Return: a reason to come back every month

## Now, next, later
Layout: timeline now-next-later
- Smarter search: Jan 2027 – Mar 2027 (Now)
- Saved lists: Feb 2027 – Apr 2027 (Now)
- One-tap reorder: Apr 2027 – Jun 2027 (Next)
- Pickup slots: May 2027 – Jul 2027 (Next)
- Member challenges: Aug 2027 – Oct 2027 (Later)
- Personal routines: Oct 2027 – Dec 2027 (Later)
> Notes: Now is funded and staffed, Next is planned, Later is the idea list; items move left as they are funded and staffed.

## Quarter by quarter
Layout: timeline quarters
- Smarter search: Jan 2027 – Mar 2027 (Find)
- One-tap reorder: Apr 2027 – Jun 2027 (Buy)
- Pickup slots: May 2027 – Jul 2027 (Buy)
- Member challenges: Aug 2027 – Oct 2027 (Return)
- Holiday release: Nov 15, 2027 (Buy)

## How confident we are
| Item | Quarter | Confidence | Depends on |
|---|---|---|---|
| Smarter search | Q1 | High | Product data clean-up |
| One-tap reorder | Q2 | High | Saved payment methods |
| Pickup slots | Q2-Q3 | Medium | Store capacity data |
| Member challenges | Q3 | Low | Loyalty rules engine |

## What we are not doing this year
- Not now: a third-party marketplace; a checkout rebuild
- Why: focus on the three themes guests asked for
