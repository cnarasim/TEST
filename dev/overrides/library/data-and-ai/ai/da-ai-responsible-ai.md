# Responsible AI

## What responsible AI covers
### Purpose
- AI guests and associates can trust
- Risks found and handled before launch
### In scope
- Principles, risk tiers and required checks
- Oversight, disclosure and incident handling
### Outcome
- Safe AI that scales with confidence
- A record of each decision and test

## Six principles for every AI solution
Layout: process list
- Fair: tested for unequal outcomes
- Transparent: people know when AI is involved
- Private: personal data used only with approval
- Secure: protected from misuse and injection
- Accountable: a named owner for each solution
- Human oversight: people approve high-stakes calls

## Checks by risk tier
| Check | Low risk | High risk |
|---|---|---|
| Evaluation before release | Basic test set | Full test set with edge cases |
| Bias testing | Not required | Required, results recorded |
| Human approval | User review of output | Required before any action |
| Disclosure | Internal note | Clear notice to guests |
| Review cadence | Yearly | Every [n] months |

## High-risk uses get a second look
Layout: swimlane handoffs
- Product team: Rates the use case risk
- Responsible AI lead: Reviews the rating
- Responsible AI lead: High risk?
- Responsible AI lead: If yes, adds legal review
- Responsible AI lead: If no, applies standard checks
- AI council: Approves launch with conditions

## KPIs that show it works
| KPI | What it measures | Data source |
|---|---|---|
| Solutions rated | Live AI with a risk rating | AI register |
| Checks complete | Required checks done before launch | AI register |
| AI incidents | Harmful or wrong outputs reported | Incident log |
| Reviews on time | Reviews done by the due date | AI register |
