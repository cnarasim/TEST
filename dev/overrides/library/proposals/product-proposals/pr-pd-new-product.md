# New product proposal

## The problem we solve
### The guest
- Who has the problem
- How often they meet it
### The problem
- What goes wrong today
- What it costs them
### The evidence
- What guests told us
- What the data shows

## Our solution
Layout: process list
- What it is: the product in one sentence
- How it works: the three things it does
- Why us: what we have that others do not

## How it works for the guest
Layout: process chevrons
- Discover: sees it in the app
- Try: a free sample in store
- Buy: one tap, pickup or delivery
- Repeat: reminders and rewards

## The opportunity
Layout: four numbers
- Market: $2.4B a year
- Guests: 6M members fit the profile
- Year-three sales: $45M
- Margin: 48% gross
> Notes: illustrative figures - every number needs its source.

## How it makes money
| Stream | How | Year 1 | Year 3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Product sales | Margin on every unit | $8M | $35M |
| Subscriptions | Monthly refills | $1M | $7M |
| Services | Paid consultations | $0.5M | $3M |

## The plan
Layout: timeline quarters
- Concept test: Jan 2027 – Mar 2027 (Product)
- Pilot in 50 stores: Apr 2027 – Jun 2027 (Stores)
- Online launch: Jul 2027 – Sep 2027 (Digital)
- National roll-out: Oct 2027 – Dec 2027 (All)

## Risks and how we handle them
| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Guests do not repeat | Medium | High | Pilot measures repeat before scaling |
| Supply not ready | Low | High | Two suppliers from the pilot |
| Cannibalises current range | Medium | Medium | Position as a new routine |

## The ask
- Decision: Approve the concept test and pilot
- Ask: $1.5M and a product team of six for nine months
- Next step: Pilot results to the board in Q3
