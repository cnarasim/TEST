# Feature proposal

## The user need
### Who
- Members who buy the same products again
### The need
- Reorder in seconds, not minutes
### Today
- Five steps, three screens, one in four give up

## What we propose
Layout: process numbered
- Remember: the products a member buys again
- Remind: when they are likely to run out
- Reorder: one tap, pickup or delivery
- Reward: points for staying on routine

## Options compared
| Criterion | Weight | One-tap reorder | Subscription | Reminder only |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Guest value | 35% | 4.5 | 4.0 | 3.0 |
| Business value | 25% | 4.0 | 4.5 | 2.5 |
| Effort (5 = least) | 25% | 4.0 | 2.5 | 4.5 |
| Risk (5 = lowest) | 15% | 4.0 | 3.0 | 4.5 |
| Weighted total | 100% | 4.2 | 3.6 | 3.5 |
> Notes: illustrative scores.

## One-tap reorder leads the weighted total
Layout: ranking
- One-tap reorder: 4.2
- Subscription: 3.6
- Reminder only: 3.5

## How we will know it works
Layout: kpi cards
- Reorder time: under 10 seconds (from 2 minutes)
- Reorder rate: 30% of members (+8 pts)
- App rating: 4.7 (+0.2)
> Notes: targets to confirm with the pilot.

## Release plan
Layout: timeline sprints
- Design: Mar 1, 2027 – Mar 12, 2027 (Product)
- Build 1: Mar 15, 2027 – Mar 26, 2027 (Engineering)
- Build 2: Mar 29, 2027 – Apr 9, 2027 (Engineering)
- Beta: Apr 12, 2027 – Apr 23, 2027 (Members)
- Release: Apr 26, 2027 (All)

## The ask
- Decision: Add one-tap reorder to next quarter's roadmap
- Ask: Two sprints of one team
- Next step: Beta with 5,000 members
