# MVP and pilot proposal

## What we believe
### Hypothesis
- Guests will book a skin consultation if it is free with a purchase
### If we are right
- Consultation guests spend 30% more over a year
### If we are wrong
- We stop after eight weeks with little cost

## The smallest test that proves it
Layout: process list
- In scope: 20 stores, booking in the app, one service
- Out of scope: paid services, new hires, store changes
- Duration: eight weeks

## The pilot plan
Layout: timeline phases
- Set up: Mar 2027 – Mar 2027
- Run: Apr 2027 – May 2027
- Read: Jun 2027 – Jun 2027

## Success criteria
| Measure | Target | Actual | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bookings per store per week | 25 | [actual] | [status] |
| Spend uplift for booked guests | 30% | [actual] | [status] |
| Guest satisfaction | 90% | [actual] | [status] |
| Associate time per booking | 20 minutes | [actual] | [status] |

## Pilot stores against control stores
Layout: matrix
- Scale: targets met in pilot, not in control - roll out
- Adjust: some targets met - change and re-test
- Stop: targets missed - stop and share the learning
- Extend: unclear results - four more weeks

## The ask
- Decision: Approve the eight-week pilot in 20 stores
- Ask: $120K and an insights analyst
- Next step: Scale decision in June
