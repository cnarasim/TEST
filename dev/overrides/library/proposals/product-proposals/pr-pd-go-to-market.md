# Go-to-market plan

## Who it is for
Layout: cards
- Primary: beauty enthusiasts who try new skincare
- Secondary: routine replenishers ready to trade up
- Not for: price-first shoppers this year

## Positioning and message
### Positioning
- The routine that adapts to your skin
### Proof
- Dermatologist tested, results in four weeks
### Message
- Your skin changes. Your routine should too.

## Channels
| Channel | Role | Share of budget |
|---|---|---|
| App and email | Members first | 30% |
| Social and creators | Reach and trial | 35% |
| Stores | Try, consult, buy | 25% |
| Search | Capture demand | 10% |

## Launch timeline
Layout: timeline countdown
- Creators seeded: Apr 1, 2027 (Marketing)
- Members early access: Apr 20, 2027 (Digital)
- Store teams trained: Apr 27, 2027 (Stores)
- Store launch: May 1, 2027 (Stores)
> Notes: example dates; the paid campaign starts May 3, two days after the store launch.

## Launch KPIs
Layout: kpi cards
- Trial: 150K guests in 8 weeks
- Repeat: 35% within 90 days
- Sales: $12M in the first quarter
> Notes: illustrative targets.
