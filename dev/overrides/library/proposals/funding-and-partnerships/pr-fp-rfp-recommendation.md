# RFP evaluation and recommendation

## What we went to market for
### The need
- The business problem in one sentence
- The capability we are buying
### The scope
- Sites, users and processes covered
- What stays out of scope
### The constraints
- Budget range and contract term
- The date the service must be live

## Six steps from long list to finalist
Layout: process chevrons
- Needs: agreed with the business
- Long list: [n] vendors invited
- RFP: questions, pricing, references
- Demos: scripted and scored by the panel
- Due diligence: security, finance, references
- Negotiate: best and final offers

## Risks of the award and how we cover them
| Risk | Mitigation | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| Delivery capacity | Named team and key people in the contract | Procurement |
| Price rises after year one | Price caps for the contract term | Category manager |
| Data and security | Security review closed before signing | IT security |
| Lock-in | Exit plan and data return clauses | Legal |
