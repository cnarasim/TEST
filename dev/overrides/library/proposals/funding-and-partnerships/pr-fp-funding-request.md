# Funding request

## What the money is for
Layout: cards
- The need: replace end-of-life store handhelds
- The outcome: faster receiving, pickup and counts
- The amount: $6.2M over two years

## The options
Layout: process list
- Do nothing: $0 now, rising repairs (Benefit: none · Risk: devices fail in peak)
- Like-for-like: $5.1M (Benefit: keeps today's speed · Risk: low)
- New devices and software: $6.2M (Benefit: 20% faster tasks · Risk: medium)

## Costs and benefits
| Category | Budget | Committed | Actual | Forecast | Variance |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Devices | $4,000,000 | $0 | $0 | $4,000,000 | $0 |
| Software | $1,200,000 | $0 | $0 | $1,200,000 | $0 |
| Roll-out and training | $1,000,000 | $0 | $0 | $1,000,000 | $0 |

## The return
Layout: four numbers
- Investment: $6.2M over two years
- Annual benefit: $3.4M from year two
- Payback: 2.1 years
- Net present value: $5.8M over five years
> Notes: illustrative figures.

## If we say no
Layout: cards
- Repairs: costs rise 40% a year
- Peak: device failures in holiday weeks
- Pickup: slower orders as volume grows

## The ask
- Decision: Approve $6.2M for new devices and software
- Ask: Release $3.5M this year
- Next step: Vendor selection in six weeks
