## What we will not cut
### Guest experience
- No change to service levels or the shelf
- Delivery promises stay as they are
### Controls
- Approvals and audits stay in place
- No cut to safety or compliance work
### Fair to teams
- Savings come from waste, not from care
- Teams help choose the changes

## Savings land over four quarters
Layout: timeline quarters
- Carrier tender: Jan 2027 to Mar 2027 (Supply chain)
- Carton change: Feb 2027 to May 2027 (Fulfilment)
- Supplier consolidation: Apr 2027 to Sep 2027 (Procurement)
- Supplies standard: Jul 2027 to Dec 2027 (Store operations)
