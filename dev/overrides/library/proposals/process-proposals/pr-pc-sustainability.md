# Sustainability proposal

## Why we should act now
### Guests and brands
- Guests ask for less packaging
- Brand partners share their own goals
### The savings
- Less energy and freight also costs less
- Waste we avoid is waste we do not pay for
### Reporting
- Investors and lenders ask for progress
- Emissions reporting rules are growing

## Where our footprint comes from
| Source | Scope | What drives it |
|---|---|---|
| Store and DC electricity | Scope 2 | Lighting, heating and cooling |
| Gas and owned vehicles | Scope 1 | Heating and any owned fleet |
| Freight to DCs, stores, guests | Scope 3 | Miles, load fill and mode |
| Products and packaging | Scope 3 | What we buy and how it is packed |
> Notes: measure each scope with the same method every year before setting targets.

## Initiatives sorted by impact and effort
Layout: matrix
- Do now: high impact, low effort - lighting and controls
- Plan: high impact, high effort - freight mode and load fill
- Quick wins: low impact, low effort - recycling at the DCs
- Later: low impact, high effort - review next year

## Four KPIs track the progress
| KPI | What it measures | Data source |
|---|---|---|
| Energy per square foot | Electricity and gas by building | Utility bills |
| Packaging per order | Packaging weight per online order | Packing data |
| Load fill | Truck space used | TMS |
| Waste diverted | Waste recycled or reused | Waste hauler reports |
