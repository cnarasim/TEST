# Policy change proposal

## Why the policy must change
### The policy today
- Approval limits set [n] years ago
- One limit per level for every kind of spend
### The problem
- Small buys wait days for senior sign-off
- Exceptions agreed by e-mail, not recorded
### The trigger
- An audit finding and a new structure
- Leaders asked for clear decision rights

## What changes, clause by clause
| Clause | Today | Proposed |
|---|---|---|
| Approval limits | One limit per level | Limits by level and spend type |
| Contract signing | Any director | Named roles only, by value |
| Exceptions | Agreed by e-mail | Logged and approved by the CFO office |
| Review | No set review | Reviewed every year |

## Requests follow the new limits
Layout: swimlane handoffs
- Requester: Raises the request in the workflow
- Workflow: Within the approver's limit?
- Approver: If yes, approve in the workflow
- Workflow: If no, route to the next level
- CFO office: Approves any exception and logs it

## Who is affected and how we help
| Group | What changes for them | Support |
|---|---|---|
| Requesters | Spend type chosen on every request | Quick guide |
| Approvers | New limits by spend type | Briefing and limit card |
| Finance | Exceptions log to keep | Workflow report |
| Audit | One record of every approval | Read access |

## The system enforces the policy
| Control | How it works | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| Limits in the workflow | Requests over a limit route up automatically | Finance systems |
| No split requests | Requests split to stay under a limit flagged | Internal audit |
| Exception log | Every exception has an approver and a reason | CFO office |
| Yearly review | Limits and roles reviewed every year | Policy owner |

## From approval to the effective date
Layout: timeline countdown
- Legal and audit review: Feb 15, 2027 (Legal)
- Policy approved: Mar 1, 2027 (Executive team)
- Workflow and training ready: Mar 22, 2027 (Finance)
- Effective: Apr 1, 2027 (All)
> Notes: example dates.

## Approve the policy and its effective date
- Decision: Approve the revised policy
- Ask: Leaders brief their teams before it starts
- Next step: Effective on [date]
