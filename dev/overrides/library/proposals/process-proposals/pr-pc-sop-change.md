# SOP and policy change

## What changes and why
### The change
- Returns without a receipt: store credit only, up to a limit
### Why
- Return abuse cost $4M last year
### Who asked
- Loss prevention and store operations

## Who is affected
| Group | What changes for them | Support |
|---|---|---|
| Guests | No refund without receipt over the limit | Clear signs, app receipts |
| Associates | New steps at the till | Training, quick guide |
| Service desk | New questions | Scripts |

## The new steps
Layout: swimlane handoffs
- Associate: Asks for receipt or order
- Associate: Receipt found?
- Associate: If yes, refunds to the original payment
- Associate: If no, offers store credit to limit
- Manager: Approves anything above the limit

## Controls
| Control | How | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| Limit per guest | System blocks above the limit | Store systems |
| Manager approval | Approval code at the till | Store managers |
| Monitoring | Weekly report of credits | Loss prevention |

## Approvals and roll-out
Layout: timeline countdown
- Legal review: Feb 15, 2027 (Legal)
- Policy approved: Mar 1, 2027 (Leadership)
- Training done: Mar 22, 2027 (Stores)
- Effective: Apr 1, 2027 (All)
