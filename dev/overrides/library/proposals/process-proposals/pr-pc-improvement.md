# Process improvement proposal

## The process today
Layout: swimlane handoffs
- Store: Finds a damaged item and emails the DC
- DC: Checks stock and replies the next day
- Store: Damage confirmed?
- Store: If yes, raises a claim form by hand
- Finance: If no, closes the request
- Finance: Re-keys the claim and credits the store a week later

## Where it hurts
Layout: kpi cards
- Cycle time: 9 days per claim
- Re-keying: 3 systems, 2 forms
- Errors: 12% of claims corrected
> Notes: illustrative figures from a two-week sample.

## The proposed process
Layout: process chevrons
- Scan: the damaged item on the handheld
- Evidence: photos attached automatically
- Approve: rules approve small claims at once
- Credit: the store is credited the same day

## Benefits
| Benefit | Measure | Baseline | Target | Actual | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Faster claims | Cycle time | 9 days | 1 day | [actual] | Store operations |
| Less effort | Hours a week | 120 | 30 | [actual] | Finance |
| Fewer errors | Claims corrected | 12% | 2% | [actual] | Finance |

## What it takes
| Item | Effort | Cost | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|
| Handheld claim screen | 6 weeks | $80K | Technology |
| Approval rules | 2 weeks | $10K | Finance |
| Training | 1 week | $15K | Store operations |

## The ask
- Decision: Approve the new claims process
- Ask: $105K and a pilot in one region
- Next step: Pilot in eight weeks
