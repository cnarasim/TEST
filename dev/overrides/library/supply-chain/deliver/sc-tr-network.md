# Supply chain network overview

## What this process covers
### Purpose
- Show how product reaches every guest
- Give every team one picture of the network
### In scope
- Stocked and flow-through paths
- Store and e-commerce fulfilment
### Outcome
- One shared map for decisions on capacity
- Clear owners for every lane

## Every node has an owner
Layout: swimlane handoffs
- Merchandising: Buys from the vendors
- Inbound logistics: Brings freight to the DCs
- DC operations: Stores and picks store orders
- Fulfilment centre: Ships online orders
- Store operations: Fills shelves and serves guests

## The systems and data it runs on
| Step | What the system does | Records it keeps |
|---|---|---|
| Sites | Every DC, store and fulfilment centre set up from a template | Site master |
| Sourcing between sites | Each store has its supplying DC | Sourcing rules |
| Transport lanes | Lanes with carriers, modes and transit times | Lanes, zones |
| Stock by site | One view of stock across the network | Stock by site |

## One network of DCs serves stores and online guests
Layout: retail supply-chain

## Controls and exceptions
| Control | How it works | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| Site master governance | New sites set up from a template | Master data team |
| Sourcing rules | Each store sourced from one primary DC | Network planning |
| Capacity review | DC capacity reviewed before each season | Supply chain leadership |
| Lane ownership | Every lane has a named owner | Logistics |

## KPIs that show it works
| KPI | What it measures | Data source |
|---|---|---|
| Network cost per unit | Total logistics cost per unit sold | Cost and volume |
| Days of supply | Stock in the network in days of sales | Stock and sales |
| Service level | Orders shipped complete and on time | Delivery data |
| DC utilisation | Capacity used per DC | Throughput vs capacity |

## Today and target
### Today
- Each team draws the network its own way
- Capacity issues found during peak
### Target
- One network model, reviewed every quarter
- Capacity planned a season ahead
