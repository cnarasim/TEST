# DC safety incidents

## What incident management covers
### Purpose
- Every associate goes home safe
- Each incident makes the next one less likely
### In scope
- Injuries, near misses, spills and damage
- Investigation and corrective actions
### Outcome
- More near misses reported, fewer injuries
- Actions closed on time and checked

## Report, secure, then find the cause
Layout: swimlane handoffs
- Associate: Reports the incident or near miss
- Supervisor: Makes the area safe
- Supervisor: Injury needing treatment?
- Supervisor: If yes, arranges first aid or care
- Supervisor: If no, logs it as a near miss
- Safety lead: Investigates the root cause
- Safety lead: Tracks the corrective action

## Every type has a first response
| Type | First response | Follow-up |
|---|---|---|
| Injury | First aid, medical care if needed | Root cause and recordable check |
| Near miss | Make the area safe | Shared at the next huddle |
| Equipment damage | Lock out the equipment | Repair and operator review |
| Spill or leak | Contain with a spill kit | Clean-up per the safety data sheet |

## Five steps after every incident
Layout: process numbered
- Report: within the shift, with photos
- Secure: area safe, equipment locked out
- Investigate: root cause, not blame
- Correct: an action with an owner and date
- Share: lessons at every shift huddle

## KPIs that show it works
| KPI | What it measures | Data source |
|---|---|---|
| Recordable rate | Recordable injuries per hours worked | Safety system |
| Near misses reported | Reports per [n] associates | Safety system |
| Actions on time | Corrective actions closed by due date | Action log |
| Training current | Associates with current training | Training records |

## Today and target
### Today
- Near misses rarely reported
- Actions agreed, then not followed up
### Target
- Near misses reported and shared weekly
- Every action closed and checked
