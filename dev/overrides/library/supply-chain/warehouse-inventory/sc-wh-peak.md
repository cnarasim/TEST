# Peak season readiness

## What peak readiness covers
### Purpose
- Every order out on time in the busiest weeks
- No surprises in labour, space or systems
### In scope
- Forecast, labour, space and equipment
- Systems, carriers and the daily peak call
### Outcome
- Same-day shipping held through peak
- A plan that adjusts each day, not each week

## Readiness counts down to peak
Layout: timeline countdown
- Peak forecast agreed: Jul 15, 2027
- Capacity plan signed: Aug 15, 2027
- Systems load tested: Sep 15, 2027
- Seasonal team trained: Oct 15, 2027
- Change freeze starts: Nov 1, 2027
- Peak weekend: Nov 26, 2027
> Notes: example dates; set them back from your own peak and freeze calendar.

## Ready means each area signed off
| Area | Ready when | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| Labour | Seasonal team hired and trained | DC operations |
| Space | Overflow storage and doors planned | DC engineering |
| Equipment | Maintenance done, spares on site | Maintenance |
| Systems | Load test passed, freeze in place | Technology |
| Carriers | Daily pickup capacity confirmed | Transportation |

## A daily peak call steers the network
Layout: swimlane handoffs
- Planning: Shares tomorrow's order forecast
- DC operations: Plans labour by shift
- DC operations: Volume over capacity?
- DC operations: If yes, adds shifts or moves work
- DC operations: If no, runs the standard plan
- Transportation: Confirms carrier pickups

## KPIs that show it works
| KPI | What it measures | Data source |
|---|---|---|
| Shipped same day | Orders out on the day they drop | Ship confirms |
| Backlog | Orders waiting past their wave | WMS order status |
| Units per labour hour | Output per hour worked | Labor management |
| Missed pickups | Carrier pickups not made | Carrier scans |
