# Adoption and readiness tracking

## What adoption tracking covers
### Purpose
- See if people are ready, then if they use it
- Act where adoption lags, store by store
### In scope
- Readiness before go-live, adoption after
- Surveys, training, usage and tickets
### Outcome
- One view of readiness and adoption
- Benefits that come from real use

## Ready first, then used, then embedded
Layout: process phases
- Ready: aware; trained; confident (Before go-live)
- Used: signing in; doing the tasks (Weeks 1-4)
- Embedded: new way only; old way off (Months 2-3)

## What we measure
| Measure | What it shows | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Awareness and confidence | People know what changes and feel ready | Pulse survey |
| Training completion | Users trained before go-live | Learning platform |
| Active use | Users doing the task the new way | System usage reports |
| Workarounds | The old way still in use | Store visits, tickets |
| How-to tickets | Where people struggle | Service desk |

## Targets at each checkpoint
| Checkpoint | Measure | Target |
|---|---|---|
| Four weeks before | Users trained | [target]% |
| Go-live | Confidence in the pulse | [target] of 5 |
| Week 2 | Active users | [target]% |
| Week 6 | Tasks done the new way | [target]% |
| Month 3 | Old way switched off | Every site |

> Notes: illustrative - the share of stores at the adoption target.

## When a site lags
Layout: swimlane handoffs
- Change lead: Flags sites below target weekly
- District manager: Finds the cause with stores
- District manager: A skills gap?
- Champion: If yes, coaches the team on shift
- Project team: If no, fixes the process or system
- Change lead: Rechecks the site next week

## The weekly adoption rhythm
Layout: process cycle
- Measure: usage, tickets, surveys
- Review: by region, district and store
- Act: coaching, fixes, messages
- Report: to the sponsor and leaders
