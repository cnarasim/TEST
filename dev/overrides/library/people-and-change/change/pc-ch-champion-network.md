# Change champion network

## What the champion network covers
### Purpose
- A trusted voice for the change in every team
- Questions answered close to the work
### In scope
- Champions in stores, DCs and support centre
- Their role, time, training and recognition
### Outcome
- Faster adoption and fewer tickets
- Feedback from the floor every week

## The champion journey
Layout: process chevrons
- Recruit: respected, curious, available
- Prepare: early access and training
- Engage: share news, gather feedback
- Support: on the floor at go-live
- Sustain: tips and refreshers after launch

## Who makes a good champion
Layout: hexagons
- Respected: peers ask them first
- Curious: likes to try new things
- Clear: explains simply
- Honest: raises bad news early
- Available: time agreed with their manager
- Spread: every store, shift and site

## How feedback flows
Layout: swimlane handoffs
- Associate: Asks or raises a problem
- Champion: Can I answer it?
- Champion: If yes, answers and shares the tip
- Change lead: If no, routes it to the project
- Project team: Answers or fixes it
- Change lead: Shares answers widely
