# Change impact assessment

## What the impact assessment covers
### Purpose
- Know who is affected, how much and when
- Plan support before people feel the change
### In scope
- Every group whose work changes
- Process, systems, roles, skills, measures
### Outcome
- An impact heatmap and a support plan
- Leaders who know what their teams face

## Six dimensions of impact
Layout: hexagons
- Process: steps, timing, hand-offs
- Systems: screens, devices, reports
- Roles: responsibilities and decisions
- Skills: what people must learn
- Measures: targets and incentives
- Place: where and when work happens

> Notes: illustrative ratings for a returns and refunds change.

## How we assess
Layout: process numbered
- List: every group the change touches
- Describe: today and target for each group
- Rate: high, medium or low impact
- Check: with the leaders of each group
- Plan: support for every high impact

## What changes for store associates
### Today
- Returns keyed in two systems, paper receipts
- A manager approves most refunds
### Target
- One screen for every return, any channel
- Approval only for exceptions, set by rules

## Support matched to impact
| Impact | Support we give | Starts |
|---|---|---|
| High | Hands-on training, floor support, champions | Six weeks before |
| Medium | E-learning, guides, leader huddle | Three weeks before |
| Low | Briefing and FAQ | One week before |

## How impacts turn into actions
Layout: swimlane handoffs
- Change lead: Runs impact sessions per group
- Group leader: Confirms the impact and timing
- Change lead: High impact?
- Change lead: If yes, builds a support plan
- Change lead: If no, covers it in communications
- Sponsor: Resolves clashes between projects
