# Roles and skills matrix

## What the skills matrix covers
### Purpose
- Know which skills we have and need
- Grow people where the gaps hurt most
### In scope
- Roles, skills and proficiency levels
- Every team member, every key skill
### Outcome
- A gap list ranked by business risk
- A development plan for every person

## Four levels of proficiency
Layout: pyramid
- 4 Expert: sets the standard, teaches others
- 3 Skilled: handles complex cases alone
- 2 Working: does routine work with little help
- 1 Aware: knows the basics, needs guidance

## Skills each role needs
| Skill | Store manager | Operations lead | Beauty advisor |
|---|---|---|---|
| Guest experience | 4 | 3 | 4 |
| Inventory and replenishment | 3 | 4 | 1 |
| Scheduling and labor | 4 | 3 | 1 |
| Product and brand knowledge | 3 | 2 | 4 |
| Reports and data | 3 | 3 | 2 |
> Notes: illustrative levels - 1 aware, 2 working, 3 skilled, 4 expert.

## People at the level the role needs
Layout: bullet
- Guest experience: 9 of 10
- Inventory and replenishment: 6 of 10
- Scheduling and labor: 7 of 10
- Reports and data: 4 of 10
> Notes: illustrative counts for one team of ten.

## Gaps ranked by risk
| Skill | Gap | Risk | Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reports and data | Six people below the level | High | Course and practice by [date] |
| Vendor negotiation | One expert, no backup | High | Pair and coach a successor |
| Inventory and replenishment | Four people below the level | Medium | Coaching from the DC team |
| Product and brand knowledge | New hires every season | Medium | Brand partner education |

## Four ways to close a gap
Layout: matrix
- Build: needed for years, time to grow it - train and coach
- Buy: needed for years, no time to grow it - hire
- Borrow: needed for a while, now - partner or contractor
- Automate: routine work - a tool or a rule does it

## Who does what
Layout: swimlane handoffs
- Employee: Rates own skills twice yearly
- Manager: Agrees ratings with the employee
- Manager: Below the role's level?
- Manager: If yes, sets a development action
- HR business partner: If no, records the rating
- HR business partner: Reports gaps by team

## Keeping the matrix current
Layout: process cycle
- Define: roles and the skills they need
- Assess: self and manager ratings
- Plan: development for each gap
- Review: twice a year, with goals
