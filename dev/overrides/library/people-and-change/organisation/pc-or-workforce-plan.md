# Workforce planning

## What workforce planning covers
### Purpose
- The people the plan needs, at the right cost
- Hiring and training started early enough
### In scope
- Stores, DCs, salon and support centre
- Headcount, skills, cost and timing
### Outcome
- A workforce plan tied to the business plan
- Gaps closed before peak and new stores

## Workforce planning in five steps
Layout: process chevrons
- Drivers: sales, stores, volume, peak
- Demand: the roles and hours we need
- Supply: the people we have, less attrition
- Gap: by role, site and month
- Actions: hire, grow, move or automate

## What drives demand
Layout: process io
- Input: Store openings and remodels
- Input: Sales and units by week
- Input: Salon service bookings
- Build: Demand model by role
- Output: Hours by role, site and week
- Output: Hiring and training plan

## The planning rhythm
Layout: process cycle
- Annual: plan with the budget
- Quarterly: refresh demand and supply
- Monthly: track hires, leavers, hours
- After peak: lessons for next year
