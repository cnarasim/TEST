# Organisation design

## What organisation design covers
### Purpose
- Shape the organisation around the strategy
- Clear roles and fewer hand-offs
### In scope
- Structure, roles, spans and layers
- Decision rights and ways of working
### Outcome
- An approved design and a plan to move
- Every person knows their role and leader

## Design in five steps
Layout: process chevrons
- Case: why the organisation must change
- Criteria: what a good design must do
- Options: two or three, scored
- Detail: roles, spans, decision rights
- Move: consult, appoint, launch

## What a good design must do
Layout: process list
- Guest first: closer to stores and guests
- Clear: one owner for each decision
- Lean: fewer layers to the front line
- Scalable: grows without a redesign
- Affordable: inside the cost envelope
- Careers: clear paths to grow

## Options scored against the criteria
| Criterion | By function | By region | Matrix |
|---|---|---|---|
| Closer to guests | Moderate | Strong | Strong |
| One owner per decision | Strong | Strong | Weak |
| Inside the cost envelope | Strong | Moderate | Weak |
| Ease of change | Strong | Moderate | Weak |
> Notes: illustrative scoring - score each option with the leadership team, then test the favourite against real decisions.

## Who decides what
| Decision | Decides | Consulted |
|---|---|---|
| Structure and layers | Executive sponsor | HR and finance |
| Role levels and pay | HR and reward | Line leaders |
| Who fills each role | Hiring leader | HR business partner |
| Timing of the moves | Program lead | Leaders and legal |

## From design to day one
Layout: timeline phases
- Design: Jan 2027 to Mar 2027 (Leadership)
- Consult and appoint: Apr 2027 to Jun 2027 (HR)
- Launch: Jul 2027 to Aug 2027 (All teams)
- Embed: Sep 2027 to Dec 2027 (Leaders)
> Notes: example dates; consultation rules and timing depend on the location and the change.

## How people move into roles
Layout: swimlane handoffs
- HR business partner: Maps people to new roles
- Line leader: Same role, little change?
- HR business partner: If yes, records it in HR
- Line leader: If no, opens a fair selection
- Line leader: Tells each person directly
- HR business partner: Updates the org chart
