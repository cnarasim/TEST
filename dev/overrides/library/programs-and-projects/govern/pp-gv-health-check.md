# Project health check and recovery

## What a health check covers
### Purpose
- An honest view of where the project stands
- Act early, while recovery is still cheap
### In scope
- Scope, time, cost, quality, risk and people
- The plan, the evidence and interviews
### Outcome
- A rating and the few things to fix
- A recovery plan the sponsor approves

## Six areas we rate
Layout: hexagons
- Scope: stable, agreed, controlled
- Schedule: realistic, critical path known
- Cost: forecast within tolerance
- Quality: defects and design under control
- Team: staffed, skilled, working well
- Stakeholders: engaged and aligned

## The health check in four weeks
Layout: process phases
- Prepare: scope; documents (Week 1)
- Review: interviews; evidence (Weeks 2-3)
- Rate: findings; ratings (Week 3)
- Recommend: actions; sponsor review (Week 4)

## Warning signs
| Sign | What it often means |
|---|---|
| Milestones move every month | The plan is not real |
| Report green, team says red | Reporting is not honest |
| Decisions wait for weeks | Governance is not working |
| The same issues every week | Root causes not addressed |

## Health by area
| Area | Finding | Rating |
|---|---|---|
| Schedule | Build three weeks behind, path unclear | Poor |
| Cost | Forecast 4% over, contingency left | Fair |
| Quality | Defect trend improving | Good |
| Team | Two key roles unfilled | Poor |
| Stakeholders | Sponsor engaged, stores not yet | Fair |
> Notes: illustrative findings - rate each area Good, Fair or Poor with the evidence.

## From health check to recovery
Layout: process chevrons
- Stabilise: freeze scope, stop new work
- Diagnose: root causes, not symptoms
- Re-plan: realistic dates and budget
- Re-baseline: the sponsor approves the plan
- Track: weekly, against the recovery plan

## Recovery options
| Option | When it fits | Trade-off |
|---|---|---|
| Cut scope | Most value sits in a few features | Some benefits come later |
| Add people | Work can be split, time is left | Cost and onboarding time |
| Move the date | Quality cannot be cut | Benefits later, more run cost |
| Pause or stop | The case no longer holds | Money spent is written off |

## How recovery is approved
Layout: swimlane handoffs
- Reviewer: Presents findings and options
- Project manager: Builds the recovery plan
- Sponsor: Business case still sound?
- Sponsor: If yes, approves the new baseline
- Steering committee: If no, decides to pause or stop
- Project manager: Reports weekly on plan
