# Vendors and statements of work

## What partner management covers
### Purpose
- Get what we pay for, on time, to standard
- One clear contract for every partner team
### In scope
- Statements of work, deliverables, changes
- Performance, invoices and exit
### Outcome
- Payments tied to accepted deliverables
- Issues raised early, through the contract

## What a good statement of work holds
Layout: hexagons
- Scope: what is in and what is out
- Deliverables: each with acceptance criteria
- Milestones: dates and payments linked
- Roles: named key people on both sides
- Assumptions: what we provide, and when
- Change: how scope and price can change

## From contract to final payment
Layout: process chevrons
- Agree: scope, price, criteria
- Mobilise: team, access, plan
- Deliver: work to the milestones
- Accept: each deliverable checked
- Pay: invoice against acceptance
- Close: handover and exit

## Deliverables and payments
| Milestone | Acceptance criteria | Payment | Accepts |
|---|---|---|---|
| Design complete | Designs approved by the design authority | 20% | Solution architect |
| Build complete | Every item built and unit tested | 30% | Technology lead |
| Testing passed | Acceptance exit criteria met | 30% | Business owners |
| Go-live and hypercare | Stable for [n] weeks after go-live | 20% | Sponsor |
> Notes: illustrative payment split - use the contract's own terms.

## How a deliverable is accepted
Layout: swimlane handoffs
- Partner: Submits the deliverable
- Reviewer: Checks it against the criteria
- Business owner: Criteria met?
- Business owner: If yes, signs the acceptance
- Partner: If no, fixes it by the date
- Vendor manager: Releases the payment

## Partner performance
| Measure | Target | Last month |
|---|---|---|
| Milestones met on time | 90% | 83% |
| Accepted at first review | 85% | 88% |
| Key people kept on the team | 95% | 100% |
| Defects found after acceptance | Under 5 | 7 |
> Notes: illustrative scorecard - agree the measures in the statement of work.

## Changes go through the contract
| Change | Route | Approves |
|---|---|---|
| Scope or deliverable | Change order with price and date | Steering committee |
| Key person swap | Notice and a handover plan | Vendor manager |
| Rates or price | Commercial review | Procurement |
| Date move at no cost | Plan update, logged | Project board |

## The governance rhythm
| Forum | How often | Covers |
|---|---|---|
| Delivery check-in | Weekly | Plan, risks, issues, actions |
| Performance review | Monthly | Scorecard, invoices, changes |
| Executive review | Quarterly | Relationship, value, renewal |
