# Stakeholder map and engagement plan

## Who has a stake
Layout: hub
- Project: the change we lead
- Executives: sponsor and leadership team
- Store teams: managers and associates
- Distribution centres: operations leaders
- Finance: budget and controls
- Technology: run and support
- Brand partners: shared processes and data

## Influence and interest
Layout: matrix
- Manage closely: high influence, high interest - shape decisions with them
- Keep satisfied: high influence, lower interest - short, regular updates
- Keep informed: lower influence, high interest - show progress, invite feedback
- Monitor: lower influence, lower interest - general updates

> Notes: illustrative register - keep names in the workbook, not on slides shared widely.

## How we engage each group
| Audience | What they need to hear | Channel | How often | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Executives | Progress, risks, decisions needed | Steering committee | Monthly | Sponsor |
| Store leaders | What changes and when, support available | Leader calls | Every two weeks | Business lead |
| Associates | What it means for their day | Huddles, short videos | At each milestone | Change lead |
| DC operations | Impact on volumes and staffing | Site visits | Monthly | Business lead |

## The engagement rhythm
Layout: process cycle
- Map: who, influence, interest
- Plan: message, channel, owner
- Engage: meetings, updates, visits
- Listen: feedback and concerns
- Adjust: the plan and the message
