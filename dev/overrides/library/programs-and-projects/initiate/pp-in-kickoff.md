# Project kick-off

## Why we are here
Layout: cards
- Purpose: one sentence on what we will change
- Guests and teams: who benefits and how
- The business: what it is worth

## What success looks like
Layout: kpi cards
- Order cycle time: under 2 hours (from 5 hours)
- Cost per order: down 15% (from $6.20)
- Guest satisfaction: 4.6 of 5 (from 4.2)
> Notes: illustrative targets - use the charter's measures.

## How we work together
| Meeting | When | Who | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| Daily stand-up | Every day, 15 minutes | Workstream teams | Progress and blockers |
| Weekly project meeting | Monday | Leads | Plan, risks, decisions |
| Steering committee | Monthly | Sponsor and leaders | Direction and approvals |
| Show and tell | Every two weeks | Everyone | What we built and learned |

## Our ground rules
- One plan: one version of the truth
- Raise it early: bad news travels fast
- Decide once: decisions logged and kept
- Owners: every action has a name and a date
- Respect time: agendas and notes for every meeting
- Help: ask, and offer

## The first thirty days
Layout: process chevrons
- Week 1: team on board, tools ready
- Week 2: current process walked through
- Week 3: requirements workshops
- Week 4: plan baselined and approved
