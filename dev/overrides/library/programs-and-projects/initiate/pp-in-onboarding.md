## Our weekly rhythm
| Meeting | When | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Daily stand-up | 9:15, 15 minutes | Progress and blockers |
| Weekly project meeting | Monday 10:00 | Plan, risks, decisions |
| Show and tell | Every other Thursday | Demo and feedback |

## Tools and documents
- Plan: the integrated plan
- Work: the team's task board
- Documents: the project site
- RAID: risks, issues, decisions
- Chat: the team channel
- Calendar: shared meetings

## Your first two weeks
Layout: process chevrons
- Day 1: access, buddy, welcome
- Days 2-3: project overview and plan
- Week 1: shadow your workstream
- Week 2: first task, first stand-up update

## Where to get help
- Access and tools: the project manager
- Process questions: the business lead
- Anything else: your buddy
