# RACI and governance

## How the project is governed
Layout: pyramid
- Executive sponsor: the outcome and the money
- Steering committee: scope, time, cost and risk decisions
- Project board: weekly plan, risks and changes within tolerance
- Workstreams: deliver, test and report

## Our forums
| Forum | Chair | Members | How often | Decides |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Steering committee | Sponsor | Business and technology leaders | Monthly | Scope, budget, go-live |
| Project board | Project manager | Workstream leads | Weekly | Plan, risks, changes in tolerance |
| Design authority | Solution architect | Leads, architects | Weekly | Design and standards |
| Workstream meeting | Workstream lead | Team | Daily or weekly | Tasks and blockers |

## Who does what
| Activity | Sponsor | Steering committee | Project manager | Workstream lead | Business owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Business case | A | R | C | I | C |
| Plan baseline | I | A | R | C | C |
| Process design | I | I | C | R | A |
| Major changes | C | A | R | C | C |
| Acceptance | I | I | C | R | A |
| Go-live | C | A | R | C | C |
> Notes: R does the work, A owns the outcome (one per activity), C is asked before, I is told after.

## What each letter means
Layout: matrix
- Responsible: does the work
- Accountable: owns the result - one person per activity
- Consulted: asked before the decision
- Informed: told after the decision

## Decision rights and tolerances
| Decision | Project board up to | Steering committee above |
|---|---|---|
| Schedule change | Two weeks on a milestone | More than two weeks |
| Budget change | 5% of a workstream budget | More than 5% |
| Scope change | No change to an objective | Any change to an objective |

## How we escalate
Layout: swimlane handoffs
- Workstream lead: Raises the issue with options
- Project manager: Assesses impact on plan and budget
- Project manager: Within the board's tolerance?
- Project manager: If yes, decides at the weekly board
- Steering committee: If no, decides at the next meeting or by email
- Project manager: Logs the decision and tells the teams
