# Resource and capacity plan

## What the resource plan covers
### Purpose
- The right people, at the right time
- Capacity gaps seen months ahead
### In scope
- Project, business and partner roles
- Demand by phase, supply and backfill
### Outcome
- A staffed plan with named owners
- Gaps closed before they hit the dates

## The team ramps with the phases
Layout: gantt
- Business analysts: Jan 2027 to Jun 2027 (Design)
- Developers: Apr 2027 to Sep 2027 (Build)
- Testers: Jun 2027 to Oct 2027 (Test)
- Trainers: Aug 2027 to Nov 2027 (Deploy)
- Hypercare team: Nov 2027 to Dec 2027 (Deploy)
> Notes: example dates; "(Phase)" names the lane, each bar is when the role is needed.

## How we close a gap
| Option | When it fits | Lead time |
|---|---|---|
| Backfill the business role | Key users needed over half their time | [n] weeks |
| Move people between projects | Portfolio priority allows it | [n] days |
| Bring in a partner | Specialist skills for a fixed period | [n] weeks |
| Re-plan the work | No one is free in time | Next board |

## How a resource request flows
Layout: swimlane handoffs
- Workstream lead: Names role, dates and skills
- Project manager: Checks plan and budget fit
- Resource manager: Someone free in time?
- Resource manager: If yes, books the person
- Project manager: If no, raises a gap with options
- Steering committee: Decides backfill, partner or delay

## Capacity risks we watch
| Risk | Early sign | Response |
|---|---|---|
| Key person dependency | One person holds a whole design | Pair and document early |
| Peak season pull-back | Store teams go back to the floor | Plan business time around peak |
| Onboarding lead time | Access takes weeks | Request access [n] weeks ahead |
| Double booking | Same people on two projects | One portfolio view of bookings |

## The monthly capacity rhythm
Layout: process cycle
- Forecast: demand by role, next six months
- Compare: against bookings and leave
- Resolve: gaps and overloads
- Confirm: bookings with line managers
