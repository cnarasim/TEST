# Communications plan

## What the communications plan covers
### Purpose
- The right message, to the right people, on time
- No one surprised on go-live day
### In scope
- Teams, leaders, brand partners and guests
- Messages, channels, timing and owners
### Outcome
- One calendar of approved messages
- Questions heard and answered

## Who hears what
| Audience | What they need to know | Channel | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|
| Store teams | What changes on the floor and when | Huddles, store portal | Store operations |
| Store and district managers | Their role, the dates, the support | Leader calls | Business lead |
| DC teams | New steps and shift plans | Site briefings | DC leaders |
| Brand partners | New portal, invoice or ASN rules | Partner portal, email | Merchant teams |
| Guests | Anything they will notice | ulta.com, the app, email | Marketing |

## Messages build to go-live
Layout: timeline countdown
- Announce: Jul 12, 2027 (Leaders)
- What changes: Aug 23, 2027 (All teams)
- Get ready: Oct 4, 2027 (Stores and DCs)
- We are live: Nov 8, 2027 (Everyone)
- How it is going: Nov 22, 2027 (Everyone)
> Notes: example dates; leaders hear each message a few days before their teams.

## One message, many channels
Layout: hub
- Core message: why, what, when, where to get help
- Leader calls: managers hear it first
- Huddles: stores and DCs, every shift
- Intranet: the full story and the FAQ
- Email: dates and actions
- Partner portal: changes for brand partners

## How a message gets out
Layout: swimlane handoffs
- Change lead: Drafts the message for an audience
- Workstream lead: Checks the facts and dates
- Sponsor: Approves leader and company notes
- Communications team: Edits and schedules it
- Leaders: Cascade it in huddles and calls
- Change lead: Collects questions and feedback

## Six rules for every message
Layout: hexagons
- Leaders first: managers before their teams
- Why first: the reason before the change
- Plain words: no project jargon
- One source: the intranet page
- Two-way: every message invites questions
- Timed: never in peak or close weeks
