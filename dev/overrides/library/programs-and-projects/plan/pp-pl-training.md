# Training plan

## What the training plan covers
### Purpose
- Every user able to work on day one
- Training that fits each role and shift
### In scope
- Stores, DCs, salon and support centre teams
- Courses, guides, practice and floor support
### Outcome
- Users trained and confident before go-live
- Completion tracked in the learning platform

## Who needs what
| Audience | What changes for them | Format | Length |
|---|---|---|---|
| Store associates | Returns and pickup screens | E-learning, practice in store | 45 minutes |
| Store managers | Approvals and new reports | Virtual leader session | 90 minutes |
| DC teams | Receiving and putaway steps | Floor training with super users | 2 hours |
| Support centre users | Finance and merchandising screens | Classroom, practice system | 1 day |
| Service desk | Common questions and fixes | Walk-through and scripts | 3 hours |
> Notes: illustrative audiences and lengths - set them from the change impact assessment.

## Training in five steps
Layout: process chevrons
- Analyse: roles, tasks, gaps
- Design: curriculum and formats
- Build: courses, guides, practice data
- Deliver: trainers first, then users
- Reinforce: floor support and refreshers

## Trainers first, then every team
Layout: pyramid
- Training lead: curriculum, standards, materials
- Master trainers: train and certify the trainers
- Trainers and super users: one per district and DC
- Users: trained in their own store or site

## Training lands close to go-live
Layout: timeline countdown
- Materials signed off: Aug 13, 2027 (Training lead)
- Trainers certified: Aug 27, 2027 (Master trainers)
- User training starts: Sep 27, 2027 (Stores and DCs)
- Training complete: Oct 22, 2027 (All users)
- Go-live: Nov 8, 2027 (Program)
> Notes: example dates; train users two to six weeks before go-live, never earlier.

## Training readiness
Layout: rings
- Store associates trained: 82%
- Managers trained: 95%
- DC teams trained: 76%
> Notes: illustrative figures from the learning platform.
