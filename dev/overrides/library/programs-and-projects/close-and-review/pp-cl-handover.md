# Handover to operations

## What the handover covers
### Purpose
- Move the solution from project to run
- No gap in support after hypercare
### In scope
- Support model, knowledge, documents, tools
- Open defects, changes and contracts
### Outcome
- Operations accept the service
- The project team can be released

## Who supports what after go-live
| Level | Who | Handles |
|---|---|---|
| First line | Service desk | Calls, how-to questions, access |
| Second line | Application support | Fixes, data issues, settings |
| Third line | Development team or partner | Code fixes and enhancements |
| Business | Process owners and super users | Process questions, training |

## Five steps to a clean handover
Layout: process chevrons
- Plan: support model and criteria
- Transfer: knowledge and shadowing
- Document: runbooks, known errors, guides
- Prove: operations run it in hypercare
- Accept: sign-off, project released

## Knowledge moves in four steps
Layout: process numbered
- Learn: walk-throughs; documents (Weeks 1-2)
- Shadow: watch the project team (Weeks 3-4)
- Reverse shadow: lead, with backup (Weeks 5-6)
- Own: support alone; project on call (Week 7)

## What changes hands
Layout: process list
- Service: the solution and its users
- Runbooks: daily, weekly and month-end tasks
- Known errors: issues and workarounds
- Monitoring: alerts and dashboards
- Access: admin roles and licences
- Contracts: partner support and renewals

## Ready for handover when
| Criterion | Evidence | Accepts |
|---|---|---|
| Stable service | No critical tickets for [n] weeks | Service owner |
| Knowledge transferred | Support team fixed tickets alone | Support lead |
| Documents complete | Runbooks and known errors reviewed | Operations manager |
| Open items agreed | Defects and changes in the backlog | Product owner |
| Monitoring in place | Alerts reach the support team | Technology operations |

## How the service is accepted
Layout: swimlane handoffs
- Project manager: Shares evidence against the criteria
- Service owner: Reviews it with support teams
- Service owner: Every criterion met?
- Service owner: If yes, accepts it into service
- Project manager: If no, extends hypercare with a plan
- Project manager: Releases the project team
