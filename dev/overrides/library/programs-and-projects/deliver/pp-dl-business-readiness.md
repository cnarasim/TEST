# Business readiness assessment

## What business readiness covers
### Purpose
- Prove the business can run on day one
- Find gaps while there is time to fix them
### In scope
- Stores, DCs, salon and support centre teams
- People, process, technology, data, support
### Outcome
- Every site rated, every gap with an owner
- Evidence for the go / no-go decision

## Six areas we assess
Layout: process list
- People: trained, roles mapped, rotas set
- Process: procedures and controls updated
- Technology: devices and access tested
- Data: master data loaded and checked
- Support: service desk and floor walkers ready
- Leadership: managers briefed and committed

## Readiness checkpoints
Layout: process phases
- Baseline: first survey; gaps (T-12 weeks)
- Checkpoint 1: actions; owners (T-8 weeks)
- Checkpoint 2: training; access (T-4 weeks)
- Final: site sign-off (T-1 week)

- Stores ready: 86%
- DCs ready: 75%
- Support teams ready: 92%
> Notes: illustrative - the share of sites rated ready at the last checkpoint.

## What each rating means
| Rating | Means | What happens next |
|---|---|---|
| Ready | Every criterion met | The site signs off |
| Ready with conditions | Gaps with a fix before go-live | Actions tracked every week |
| Not ready | Gaps that stop the business | Escalation and a recovery plan |

## From survey to sign-off
Layout: swimlane handoffs
- Site leader: Completes the checklist
- Change lead: Scores the results by area
- Change lead: Any area not ready?
- Workstream lead: If yes, agrees actions and dates
- Change lead: If no, records the site as ready
- Business owner: Signs off for go-live
