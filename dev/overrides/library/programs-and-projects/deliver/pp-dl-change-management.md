# Change management and training

## What changes for whom
| Group | What changes | Impact | Support they need |
|---|---|---|---|
| Store associates | New screens for pickup and returns | High | Hands-on training, quick guides |
| Store managers | New dashboards and approvals | Medium | Leader session, champions |
| DC teams | New receiving steps | High | Floor training, super users |
| Finance | New close reports | Low | Briefing and guide |

## From awareness to adoption
Layout: process chevrons
- Aware: why it changes
- Understand: what it means for me
- Able: trained and practised
- Adopt: using it every day
- Sustain: the new normal

## Training plan
| Audience | Format | When | Length | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Store associates | E-learning and practice in store | Three weeks before | 45 minutes | Change lead |
| Store managers | Virtual leader session | Four weeks before | 90 minutes | Business lead |
| DC super users | Classroom, train the trainer | Six weeks before | 2 days | Trainer |
| Service desk | Walk-through and scripts | Two weeks before | 3 hours | Support lead |

## A champion in every team
Layout: process list
- Champions: one per store and shift
- Prepare: early access and training
- Share: tips and quick guides
- Listen: questions and concerns
- Escalate: issues to the project
- Celebrate: wins and good practice

## Readiness by group
Layout: bullet
- Store associates trained: 82 of 100
- Store managers trained: 95 of 100
- DC super users ready: 100 of 100
- Service desk briefed: 90 of 100

## How we measure adoption
| Measure | Target | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Users active in week one | 95% | System usage |
| Tasks done the new way | 90% by week four | Process reports |
| Confidence score | 4 of 5 | Pulse survey |
| How-to tickets | Down 50% by week four | Service desk |
