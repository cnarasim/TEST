# Sprint review and demo

## What the sprint review covers
### Purpose
- Show working software, not slides
- Get feedback while change is cheap
### In scope
- The stories finished in this sprint
- Sprint goal, demo, feedback, next steps
### Outcome
- Accepted stories and an updated backlog
- Stakeholders who know what comes next

## The review in one hour
Layout: process numbered
- Goal: what we set out to do (5 min)
- Demo: working software, real scenarios (30 min)
- Feedback: what to change or add (15 min)
- Backlog: what comes next (5 min)
- Close: decisions and actions (5 min)

## The sprint in numbers
Layout: kpi cards
- Stories finished: 18 of 21
- Points delivered: 42 against a plan of 40
- Defects found in the demo: 3 minor
> Notes: illustrative figures from the work management tool.

## What we showed
| Story | What the user can now do | Accepted |
|---|---|---|
| Check stock nearby | See shade stock in nearby stores | Yes |
| Hold for pickup | Hold an item for 24 hours | Yes |
| Pickup alert | Get a text when the order is ready | No |
| Associate view | See today's holds at the counter | Yes |
> Notes: example stories - the product owner accepts against each story's criteria.

## Done means done
Layout: hexagons
- Built: code peer reviewed
- Tested: automated tests pass
- Accepted: criteria met, product owner agrees
- Secure: security checks clean
- Documented: user and release notes
- Releasable: ready for the next release

## From feedback to backlog
Layout: swimlane handoffs
- Stakeholder: Gives feedback on the demo
- Scrum master: Logs it in the backlog
- Product owner: Worth doing now?
- Product owner: If yes, ranks it for next sprint
- Product owner: If no, parks it with a reason
- Team: Plans the next sprint

## Who comes and why
| Who | Role in the review | Brings |
|---|---|---|
| Product owner | Accepts stories, ranks the backlog | Business priorities |
| Delivery team | Shows the work, answers questions | The working software |
| Business stakeholders | Try it, give feedback | How the work is really done |
| Scrum master | Keeps time, captures actions | The review notes |
