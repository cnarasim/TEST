# Hypercare

## Hypercare in four numbers
Layout: four numbers
- Duration: 4 weeks after go-live
- Critical response: 30 minutes, day and night
- Floor walkers: 40 in stores and DCs
- Exit: 5 days in a row with no critical ticket

## How a user gets help
Layout: swimlane handoffs
- User: Raises the question or problem
- Floor walker: Answers how-to questions on the spot
- Service desk: Logs and routes every ticket
- Hypercare team: Known fix?
- Hypercare team: If yes, applies it and closes the ticket
- Project team: If no, finds the cause and fixes it
- Service desk: Tells the user and closes the ticket

## The daily rhythm
Layout: process cycle
- Morning stand-up: new tickets and priorities
- Fix: an owner on every critical item
- Midday check: progress and escalations
- Evening report: status to leaders

## Response targets
| Priority | Example | Respond | Resolve |
|---|---|---|---|
| Critical | Stores cannot sell, orders stop | 30 minutes | 4 hours |
| High | A process is slow or has a workaround | 2 hours | 1 day |
| Medium | A report is wrong | 1 day | 5 days |
| Low | A question or cosmetic issue | 2 days | Next release |

## Tickets this week
Layout: kpi cards
- Opened: 214 (down from 380)
- Resolved: 236 (110% of opened)
- Open critical: 0 (from 3)
> Notes: illustrative figures from the service desk.

## When hypercare ends
| Criterion | Target | Actual | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Open critical tickets | 0 for 5 days | 0 for 3 days | Not met |
| New tickets a day | Under 30 | 34 | Not met |
| Business processes on time | 100% | 100% | Met |
| Support team trained | 100% | 100% | Met |

## Handing over to the run team
Layout: process chevrons
- Knowledge: documents and training
- Tickets: open items transferred
- Monitoring: alerts and owners
- Sign-off: run team accepts
