# Cash forecasting

## What cash forecasting covers
### Purpose
- Know the cash position weeks ahead
- Fund the holiday stock build without surprises
### In scope
- Receipts and payments, 13 weeks out
- Actuals against forecast, funding decisions
### Outcome
- A weekly forecast leaders trust
- Liquidity kept above the minimum

## Teams supply the flows, treasury decides
Layout: swimlane handoffs
- Business teams: Submit expected receipts and payments
- Treasury analyst: Builds the 13-week forecast
- Treasury analyst: Cash above the minimum?
- Treasury analyst: If yes, invest or pay down debt
- Treasurer: If no, draw on the credit line
- Treasury analyst: Compares actuals, explains gaps

## Each cash flow has a source and an owner
| Cash flow | Forecast source | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| Store and online sales | Sales plan and settlement lag | Treasury |
| Merchandise payments | Open payables and planned receipts | Accounts payable |
| Payroll and benefits | Payroll calendar and headcount | HR and payroll |
| Rent and taxes | Lease schedules, tax calendar | Real estate and tax |
| Capital spend | Capital plan by month | FP&A |

## The forecast rolls forward every week
Layout: process cycle
- Actuals: last week's cash in and out
- Update: receipts and payments by source
- Roll: add a week, 13 weeks out
- Review: funding needs with the treasurer
- Explain: variances by line

## Systems and data for cash forecasting
| Step | What the system does | Records it keeps |
|---|---|---|
| Bank balances | Balances pulled from every bank | Daily cash position |
| Payables | Payments due by week | Open payables |
| Receipts | Expected sales and settlements | Receipts forecast |
| Forecast model | 13 weeks of receipts and payments | Forecast versions |
| Variance | Forecast vs actual by line | Variance report |

## Controls protect liquidity
| Control | How it works | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| Minimum liquidity | Cash and unused credit kept above a set floor | Treasurer |
| Forecast sign-off | Weekly forecast reviewed before release | Treasury manager |
| Variance review | Gaps above [threshold] explained every week | Treasury analyst |
| Bank transfer control | Two approvals for transfers and credit draws | Treasury operations |

## KPIs show how good the forecast is
| KPI | What it measures | Data source |
|---|---|---|
| Forecast accuracy | Forecast vs actual cash, by week | Forecast versions |
| Liquidity headroom | Cash plus unused credit | Bank and credit balances |
| Idle cash | Cash above the operating need | Daily cash position |
| Forecast bias | Forecast too high or low over time | Variance history |

## From monthly spreadsheets to weekly rolls
### Today
- Forecast built monthly in spreadsheets
- Holiday stock build funded at short notice
### Target
- A 13-week forecast refreshed every week
- Funding planned weeks before the peak
