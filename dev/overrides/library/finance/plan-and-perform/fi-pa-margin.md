# Gross margin analysis

## What gross margin analysis covers
### Purpose
- Explain every move in gross margin
- Point merchants to the levers that matter
### In scope
- Margin by category, brand and channel
- Markup, promotions, vendor funds, freight, shrink
### Outcome
- A monthly margin bridge leaders trust
- Actions on the drivers that slipped

## FP&A explains, merchants act
Layout: swimlane handoffs
- Accounting: Posts sales, cost and adjustments
- FP&A: Builds the margin bridge
- FP&A: Margin on plan?
- FP&A: If yes, publish the margin pack
- Merchants: If no, explain and plan actions
- Leadership: Reviews actions every month

## Five drivers explain the margin change
| Driver | What moves it | Who owns it |
|---|---|---|
| Mix | Category, brand and channel share | Merchants |
| Initial markup | Cost and ticket price at the buy | Merchandise planning |
| Promotions and markdowns | Depth and length of offers | Pricing and promotions |
| Vendor funds | Allowances and rebates earned | Vendor funds team |
| Freight and shrink | Inbound cost and inventory loss | Supply chain, loss prevention |
> Notes: add loyalty rewards and any occupancy costs if your margin definition includes them.

## Systems and data for margin analysis
| Step | What the system does | Records it keeps |
|---|---|---|
| Sales | Sales, discounts, returns by item | Sales history |
| Cost of goods | Item cost with freight and duty | Cost of goods postings |
| Vendor funds | Funds earned by vendor and item | Vendor fund accruals |
| Markdowns | Price changes valued by item | Markdown history |
| Margin model | Bridge vs plan and last year | Margin bridge |

## The margin review repeats every month
Layout: process cycle
- Close: actuals after month end
- Bridge: change vs plan and last year
- Explain: each driver has an owner
- Act: price, promotion, cost or mix
- Track: actions in next month's bridge

## Controls keep one margin number
| Control | How it works | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| Cost of goods reconciliation | Item cost agrees to the ledger | Cost accounting |
| Vendor fund accrual review | Accruals agree to signed agreements | Vendor funds team |
| Markdown approval | Markdowns approved within limits | Merchandising leaders |
| One margin definition | Same margin formula in every report | FP&A |

## KPIs show margin and its drivers
| KPI | What it measures | Data source |
|---|---|---|
| Gross margin rate | Gross margin as a share of net sales | Ledger |
| Initial markup | Planned margin at the ticket price | Orders and prices |
| Markdown rate | Markdowns as a share of sales | Price change history |
| Margin vs plan | Basis points over or under plan | Plan vs actual |
