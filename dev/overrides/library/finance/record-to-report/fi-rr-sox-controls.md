# Controls testing and audit readiness

## What controls testing covers
### Purpose
- Prove the controls behind the numbers work
- Be ready when the auditors arrive
### In scope
- Key controls in every financial process
- Walkthroughs, testing and remediation
### Outcome
- No surprises at year-end testing
- Gaps fixed before they matter

## Owners run controls, internal audit tests
Layout: swimlane handoffs
- Control owner: Runs it, keeps evidence
- Internal audit: Tests a control sample
- Internal audit: Control worked as designed?
- Internal audit: If yes, conclude it is effective
- Control owner: If no, fix the gap and retest
- Controller: Reports to the audit committee

## The control matrix links risks to evidence
| Risk | Key control | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Vendor paid twice | Duplicate invoice check | Exception report, review |
| Early or late revenue | Cut-off review | Cut-off test, journals |
| Inventory misstated | Stock-to-ledger reconciliation | Count results, reconciliation |
| Unapproved journal | Manual journal approval | Journal with approver |
| Unauthorized access | User access review | Signed access review |

## The testing year runs in four phases
Layout: process phases
- Scope: risks; key controls; materiality (Q1)
- Walk through: design; evidence (Q2)
- Test: interim samples; fixes (Q3)
- Roll forward: year-end tests; sign-off (Q4)

## Systems and data for controls testing
| Step | What the system does | Records it keeps |
|---|---|---|
| Control library | Risks and controls by process | Risk and control matrix |
| Evidence | Owners upload proof as they work | Control evidence |
| Testing | Samples, steps and results | Test workpapers |
| Deficiencies | Gaps rated and tracked | Deficiency log |
| Reporting | Status by process for leaders | Control status report |

## Deficiencies are rated by severity
| Rating | What it means | Who hears |
|---|---|---|
| Control deficiency | A control gap that does not prevent or detect an error in time | Process owner, internal audit |
| Significant deficiency | Less than a material weakness, but merits oversight attention | Audit committee |
| Material weakness | A reasonable possibility of a material misstatement | Audit committee, investors |
> Notes: a material weakness is disclosed in the annual report; rate deficiencies with the external auditor's framework.

## KPIs show we are ready for the audit
| KPI | What it measures | Data source |
|---|---|---|
| Tests on plan | Tests done by the plan date | Testing plan |
| Test exceptions | Tests with an exception | Test results |
| Open deficiencies | Deficiencies not yet fixed | Deficiency log |
| Days to remediate | Finding to successful retest | Remediation dates |

## From year-end scramble to testing all year
### Today
- Evidence chased at year end
- Testing tracked in spreadsheets and e-mail
### Target
- Evidence stored when the control runs
- One control library, tested all year
